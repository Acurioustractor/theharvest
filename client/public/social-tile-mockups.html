Tracking Architecture — GHL + UTM

Every link in every post uses this format:

theharvestwitta.com.au/whats-on?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=first-gathering&utm_content=post-1-announcement

What this gives you:

  • utm_source — which platform (instagram, facebook, google-business)
  • utm_medium — always "social" for organic posts
  • utm_campaign — always "first-gathering" for this event
  • utm_content — which specific post (post-1-announcement, post-3-shaun, etc.)

GHL tracking flow:

  • Person sees post → clicks link → lands on /whats-on with UTM params
  • Person fills RSVP form → creates GHL contact with source = utm_source
  • GHL shows: which posts drive RSVPs, which platform works best

Setup steps:

  • 1. Connect FB + IG in GHL (dashboard → Marketing → Social Planner)
  • 2. Add UTM capture to the Harvest RSVP form
  • 3. Schedule posts through GHL with UTM-tagged links
  • 4. After event: pull GHL report → see attribution
