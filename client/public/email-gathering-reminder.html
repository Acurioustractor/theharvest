Free · No tickets · Just come

What's happening

Shaun's Oysters

Fresh from Moreton Bay, shucked on the lawn. Shaun Fisher is a Quandamooka oyster farmer — come hear his story and taste some of the best oysters on the coast.

Milk Crate Pavilion

We've collected donated dairy crates from across the Blackall Range. On Saturday, we build them into something together. Bring your hands — we'll bring the crates.

Photo Wall

A growing portrait series of the people and places of this ridge. Come see the first prints — and maybe become part of it.

See the Building

Rammed earth walls, timber floors, a fireplace, and hinterland views. Walk through the space that's becoming The Harvest — and tell us what you'd build here.

Music & Drinks

Live music on the lawn. Local drinks. Good conversation. Nothing fancy — just neighbours getting to know each other.

What to bring

A chair or a blanket. Your kids. Your neighbours. Or nothing at all.
