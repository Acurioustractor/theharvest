## 2026 Focus Areas

### 1. Micro-Enterprise and Maker Pathways
- Support local makers transitioning from hobby to business
- Build repeatable workshop and market operations
- Test sustainable pricing and scheduling

### 2. Community Connection
- Monthly dinners continue as relationship infrastructure
- Seasonal programming tied to land rhythms
- Working bees as community-building, not just maintenance

### 3. Sustainable Enterprise
- Revenue from venue hire, markets, workshops
- Revenue reinvested in land care and community programs
- Testing what enterprise models work for a small rural community

### 4. Governance Development
- Community commons model being designed
- Community land trust exploration
- Shared decision-making with local stakeholders

### 5. Storytelling
- Capture first Harvest vignettes for compendium
- Monthly dinner stories, market stories, seasonal stories
- Connect Harvest narratives to broader ACT philosophy
