## Land Acknowledgment

The Harvest sits on Jinibara Country. This is not a formality — it shapes how the space is used, who is centred, and what kind of enterprise is appropriate. Land acknowledgment is embedded in the site's identity, not just its footer.

## Governance Model

The Harvest is moving toward a community commons model:
- Shared governance with local community
- Community land trust structure being explored
- Revenue from enterprise reinvested in land care and community programs
- ACT as steward, not owner — the goal is community self-governance
