# The Harvest — Stories

The Harvest does not yet have linked vignettes in the ACT compendium. This is a storytelling opportunity.

## Storytelling Priorities

Stories to capture from The Harvest's work:

### Monthly Dinner Conversations
The regular ACT dinners at The Harvest are where relationships form and community direction emerges. Capturing the texture of these evenings — who comes, what's discussed, what shifts — would ground the Harvest's purpose in lived experience.

### Farm Working Bee Reflections
Working bees bring people onto the land. The conversations that happen while weeding, planting, and building carry as much value as the physical work. Stories from these moments connect land practice to community.

### Market Stallholder Experiences
Local makers and growers who sell at Harvest markets have their own enterprise stories. Their experience of building a micro-business through the Harvest platform is worth documenting.

### Visiting Partner Impressions
When partners from other ACT projects visit The Harvest, they see the philosophy made physical. Their reflections on what it feels like to be in this place would strengthen the compendium.

### Seasonal Gathering Stories
The rhythm of seasons at The Harvest — what grows, what's harvested, what's celebrated — tells the story of a community tied to place.

## When Stories Are Captured

New vignettes will be added to this folder following the standard template:
- YAML frontmatter with consent scope, ALMA signals, LCAA stage
- Story summary (200-400 words)
- ALMA reflection table
- Media needs
- Consent and cultural notes
