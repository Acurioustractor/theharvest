# The Harvest — Identity

> Community hub for CSA programs and seasonal gatherings on Jinibara Country.

## Purpose

The Harvest is an old nursery in Witta, now a small enterprise hub on Jinibara Country in the Sunshine Coast Hinterland. It's where local makers, growers, and community members come together through markets, workshops, venue hire, and seasonal gatherings. The Harvest is not just a venue — it is belonging tested in markets, meals, and shared work.

This is ACT's grounded enterprise — the place where philosophy meets practice, and community connection generates sustainable revenue.

## Philosophy Alignment

| Principle | How It Shows Up |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Country sets the pace** | Land-based seasonal programming. Activity scaled to what the site can carry. |
| **Identity before product** | Events are culture, not marketing. Gatherings count as outputs if they strengthen trust. |
| **Enterprise funds the commons** | Revenue from venue hire, markets, and workshops funds land care and community value. |
| **Build for handover** | Community commons model — shared governance in development. |
| **Community authority comes first** | Local makers and growers shape the program. ACT stewards, community leads. |

## LCAA in Practice

| Stage | How It Manifests |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Listen** | Monthly dinners for listening, connection, relationship-building. Community feedback shapes programming. |
| **Curiosity** | Testing enterprise models — what works for Witta? CSA, markets, workshops, venue hire. |
| **Action** | Regular events, maker pathways, local business directory, venue operations. |
| **Art** | Seasonal gatherings become cultural expression. The Harvest itself is an artwork of community. |

## Brand Voice

- Local, warm, seasonal
- "Community hub" not "event venue"
- Centre the Witta community and local producers
- Acknowledge Jinibara Country with genuine respect
- Celebrate what's grown locally

## The Land

The Harvest sits on **Jinibara Country** in the Sunshine Coast Hinterland. The site includes:
- Pasture under regenerative grazing
- Food forest and orchard
- Native corridor restoration
- Water catchment systems
- Studio and gathering buildings
