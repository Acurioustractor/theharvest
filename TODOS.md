## Community Governance Formation

**Priority:** P2
**Owner:** Ben + Nic
**Decision deadline:** 31 March 2027 (structure decided)
**First meeting deadline:** 30 June 2027 (aligned with first-option renewal decision on lease)

**What:** Decide the shape of community governance at The Harvest — what body, what mandate, what composition, what decision scope.

**Why:** Strategic plan's Year-3 theme is "Hand over the keys" (community cooperative takes operational lead). That trajectory requires a Year-1/Year-2 seed. Cannot be decided pre-commencement because the shape depends on who actually engages with the site in the first 6 months of operations. Committing to a body now risks designing for imagined community instead of the actual one that shows up.

**Pros of deciding now vs deferring:** Ends ambiguity about what "community governance formed" means. Community reads the plan and sees a seat at the table is real.

**Cons of deciding now:** Designing for imagined community; likely to need restructuring in Year 2 when real engagement data arrives.

**Context:** Plan (line ~89) lists "Community governance formed" as a Q4 2026 bullet without shape. Year 3 theme: "Community cooperative takes operational lead." The CEO review (D6, 2026-04-24) resolved this as defer-with-date rather than specify-now.

**Depends on / blocked by:**
- First 6 months of programming + Art Space + garden engagement (Jul-Dec 2026) to see who's engaged enough to serve
- Sub-op signed (as they may be part of the governance conversation)

**Options to evaluate when decision deadline hits:**
- Board of Advisors (informal, appointed)
- Community Reference Group (elected, advisory)
- Open Community Assembly (town-hall-style, quarterly)
- Hybrid (board + assembly)
- Formal cooperative (members, shares, board — aligns with Year-3 theme)

## Year-2 Foundation / DGR Pathway

**Priority:** P3
**Owner:** Ben (research lead), Nic (philanthropic relationship leverage)
**Deadline:** Month 9 of commencement (approx. March 2027)

**What:** Set up the mental model for a Harvest-specific foundation or DGR-endorsed vehicle that major philanthropic funders can engage with. Covers: entity structure options (company limited by guarantee, public ancillary fund, DGR endorsement pathway), relationship to Harvest Pty (sister entity vs shared governance vs covenant), Luff involvement opportunity (as founder philanthropists), first funder targets.

**Why:** Year-2+ major philanthropic funders typically require DGR (Deductible Gift Recipient) status to fund. Harvest Pty is a for-profit Pty Ltd; it cannot receive tax-deductible donations. Without a DGR pathway in hand, major funders default to working with established DGRs (like ACT if ACT is structured that way, or external trusts). Setting up the pathway in Year-1 ensures Harvest has the option in Year-2.

**Pros:** Unlocks philanthropic funding channel at Year-2 when Harvest can show traction. Gives Luffs a pathway to deepen involvement as philanthropists if desired. Opens reproducible "community-hub-with-Foundation" pattern for other sites.

**Cons:** Legal + accounting complexity. Requires capable board governance (which may depend on community governance decision landing first). Premature if Harvest-Pty operations are still unstable at Month 9.

**Context:** `thoughts/wiki/operations/harvest-pty-incorporation-and-sequencing.md` §2 notes: "Future charity pathway — if a major philanthropic funder later wants a DGR, a Harvest-specific foundation vehicle can sit alongside without restructuring ACT." Directionally planned; not yet designed.

**Depends on / blocked by:**
- Harvest Pty operational stability (Month 6+ trading data)
- Standard Ledger engagement including charity/DGR advisory
- At least one concrete philanthropic funder conversation to shape what the vehicle needs to do
