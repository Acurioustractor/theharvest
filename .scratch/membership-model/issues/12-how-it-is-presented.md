# How it is presented

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 05, 07

## Question

How is the model explained so it reads as an invitation rather than a paywall?

Build a rough, cheap artifact to react to — a draft membership page or one-pager, not
production copy. Use /prototype, and read the act-voice skill before writing a word.

Explore in the prototype:
- Whether price leads or follows the story.
- How the free boundary from "What stays free forever" is stated up front, so the community
  claim survives contact with a price.
- How two tiers are shown without a comparison grid that makes a community hub look like a
  SaaS product.
- Whether effort-as-currency, if adopted, is presented as an equal door or a lesser one.
- Wording worth stealing from the comparable-models research, adapted to Harvest voice.
- What the sauna is called, given a members' sauna and a community sauna are different objects.

Constraints: no em-dashes, no AI vocabulary, "work days" not "working bees", no invented
figures, no price named publicly until this map closes. Link the prototype from this ticket
rather than pasting it in.

## Context from resolved tickets

From [Comparable models](01-comparable-models-worldwide.md): the presentational move worth
stealing is School Farm CSA's sliding scale — four prices, identical product, each band labelled
by what the money lets the farm do, not what the buyer gets. Self-assessed, no means test, no
resentment. Pair it with the CERES pattern of keeping the free-access line in the header of the
pricing page itself.
