# Platform choice

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 07, 08

## Question

Where does membership actually live and bill: Mighty Networks, Square, GoHighLevel, Stripe
direct, or a combination?

This is where the long-running Mighty question finally gets answered. The funnel currently
runs home to /membership to Mighty, and the 2026-07-06 rule that every members-page mention
should open Mighty directly was superseded on 2026-07-29.

Press on:
- What does Mighty genuinely earn its place doing, once billing may live elsewhere? Community
  space, or habit?
- Does membership state need to reach GHL for tags and automations, and how much glue is
  acceptable before the integration becomes its own maintenance burden?
- Can a member be identified in person, at a sauna door, without a laptop?
- Who operates this when Ben is overseas? Simplicity beats capability if the alternative is a
  system only one person can run.
- What is the cost of being wrong, and how hard is it to move later?
