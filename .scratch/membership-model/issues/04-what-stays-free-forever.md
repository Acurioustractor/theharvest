# What stays free forever

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Ben (claimed 2026-07-31)
Resolved: 2026-07-31
Blocked by: 03

## Question

What can anyone in Witta do at The Harvest, without paying and without joining, permanently?

Settle this as a values boundary before pricing, so price is drawn against a fixed line
rather than the line being whatever is left after pricing.

Press on:
- Walking in, being in the garden, using the grounds. Is public access unconditional?
- Community days, shared meals, the weekend pizza rhythm. Free, paid, or member-first?
- Is there a promise here The Harvest would not break even if money were tight? Name it, and
  be willing to have it quoted back.
- Where does this boundary come from — the lease, the co-design ethos, the Butterfly or DGR
  relationship, or Ben and Nicholas's own line? Different sources bind differently.
- What would have to go wrong for this boundary to move, and who gets to move it?

## Context from resolved tickets

From [Comparable models](01-comparable-models-worldwide.md): two mechanisms make this concrete.
Swansea Hackspace states on its pricing page that "Attendance at the regular open nights is
without charge and open to all" — one named recurring free session, held forever. CERES keeps
"CERES is a public park that is free to visit" in the header of every page including the pricing
page. Both cost nothing and stop a paywall from eating the community claim. Consider naming one
recurring free session as the answer to this ticket rather than a general principle.

From [One tier or two](03-one-tier-or-two.md): this ticket now has a named job. Ben committed
to a **floor** — a set of things free always includes, which does not get thinned to drive
conversion, because free governs when free and funnel conflict. Specifying that floor IS this
ticket.

Two constraints inherited:
- The cut is **marginal cost**, not perceived value. Anything with zero cost per use is a
  candidate for the floor and can be given without limit. Anything carrying a dollar figure
  every time it is used is not, and belongs in 05.
- The floor must be quotable. It gets committed to publicly and has to survive being read
  back during a tight month.

## Resolution (2026-07-31, grilled with Ben)

### The floor, two lines

> **1. The garden and grounds are free and open to anyone during opening hours.**
> No joining, no paying, no carve-outs.
>
> **2. There is always one named recurring session that costs nothing to attend.**
> Currently the Friday movie night.

Two lines, deliberately. Short enough that anyone in Witta can quote it back, which is the
whole point of a floor.

### 1. Public access is unconditional

Free and unconditional during opening hours, bounded by **hours and hazard supervision, never
by payment or membership**. Nothing carved out of the grounds from day one.

The distinction that matters and that a broker will ask about: *unconditional* is not
*unsupervised*. Free access to the garden is settled. Free access to a live fire is a
different question, and staffing the oven reads as safety rather than as a paywall.

Grounded in: CERES keeps "a public park that is free to visit" in every page header including
the pricing page (ticket 01); lease permitted use 1.3 leads with "community hub and gathering
space"; and under the marginal-cost rule from ticket 03 a person standing in the garden
consumes nothing, so there is nothing to price.

### 2. Pizza is paid, and open to all

Not free, not member-first, as far as the public is concerned.

- ~$8.00 of goods per pizza is real marginal cost, so ticket 03's rule puts it on the paid side.
- It is the engine, not a nice-to-have: **$6,108 of the $8,279 Base contribution, 74% of
  everything the place keeps**.
- Open-to-all-at-a-price protects the community claim better than member-first, because pizza
  is the first thing a newcomer meets and it would define the place.

**Whether MEMBERS get it free or discounted is ticket 05, not this one.** Ben raised free
member pizza during this grilling and it is a live idea, carried forward with numbers.

### 3. The commitment is fixed; the instance is swappable

Ben's qualifier was that the Friday movie night "can change". Resolved by separating promise
from vehicle:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fixed forever** | Grounds open and free; there is always one free recurring session |
| **Swappable, with notice** | Which session it is. Friday movie is the current instance |
| **Never allowed** | Dropping it without replacing it, or letting it quietly lapse |

The sentence to be quoted back is not "Friday movie night forever". It is **"there is always a
free way in, and right now it's Friday."** Swansea promises open nights, not a particular film.

**Who can move it: both founders together.** Not one alone, and specifically not one alone
under revenue pressure, because that is the exact circumstance the floor defends against. A
commitment erodes when one person decides in a tight month, not when two people choose to
change it.

### 4. Where the boundary comes from, and what makes it bind

Not the lease. Permitted use 1.3 supports free access but does not mandate it, and Sonas has
no obvious lever to enforce one. Not DGR, which runs through Butterfly and does not reach
Harvest. So this is **Ben and Nicholas's own line**.

That is the weakest source on paper, and naming it plainly matters, because it means the
binding mechanism has to be **publication**. A promise on the site that any neighbour can
quote back binds harder than a private intention. If the floor stays internal it is not a
floor, it is a preference. This is a hard requirement on
[How it is presented](12-how-it-is-presented.md).

### Considered and not chosen

**Work days as the named free session.** Already free, and they fit the co-design ethos better
than anything else on the site. Rejected because they *ask* rather than *offer*: "the thing we
promise the town for free is that you can come and work" is a strange sentence. They stay
free; they are not the promise.

### Flagged during this grilling, for other tickets

- **Free pizza for members** — Ben's idea. Costs $26.79 per pizza in swing (not a discount, a
  sign flip: no revenue, goods still bought), against $4.02 for the 15% discount. A weekly
  member taking one a week costs ~$1,393/yr, implying a fee near **$28/week** to stand still,
  which lands almost exactly on the parked $30/wk figure. Carried to
  [What sits behind the paywall](05-what-sits-behind-the-paywall.md).
- **"Info lands with members first"** is already live on the home page. A shipped member-first
  practice sitting next to an unconditionally-public claim. Also carried to 05.
- **Oven capacity** is the sauna-capacity problem arriving early on a different asset. Carried
  to [Sauna capacity against unlimited access](11-sauna-capacity.md).
