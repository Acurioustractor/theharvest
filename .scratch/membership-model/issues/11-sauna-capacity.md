# Sauna capacity against unlimited access

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 05

## Question

If members use the sauna free, how is a physically small facility rationed on the day everyone
wants it?

An unlimited promise against a capacity-limited object fails on its first cold Saturday, and
it fails publicly, in front of the neighbours it was meant to serve.

Press on:
- How many people fit, for how long, and how many sessions exist in a week? The promise has to
  be sized against that number, not against goodwill.
- Booking, first come, or fixed sessions? Who runs the roster?
- What happens when a member is turned away? That is the moment the model is judged.
- Are there member hours and public hours, and does that split solve the rationing and the
  revenue question at once?
- Does the answer here change the free-versus-paid boundary set earlier? If so, say so plainly
  rather than patching it here.
