## Brand spine

The Harvest is a garden, kitchen, and art space taking shape in Witta.

Launch line:

```text
A working place for growing, feeding, making, and gathering in Witta.
```

## The three rooms

| Room | Verb | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garden | Grow | food, plants, kids, working bees, soil |
| Kitchen | Feed | long tables, pop-ups, milk bar, fire cooking |
| Art Space | Make | residencies, workshops, repair, exhibition |

## First residency theme

```text
timber, dairy, and co-operatives
```

This is the launch story.

It should show up in the pavilion, website, launch deck, captions, local asks, signage, and conversations with timber, dairy, and co-op people.

## Launch target

Working target: **20 June 2026**.

Treat this as the launch readiness date for brand, website, deck, and comms. The operational go/no-go still needs checking before any public event promise is made.

## Voice

Write like someone standing at the gate.

Use:

- plain sentences
- named rooms
- named objects
- local detail
- practical asks
- one clear invitation

Avoid:

- "we are excited to announce"
- "vibrant community hub"
- polished venue language
- startup gradients
- big claims before the place can hold them

## Visual cues

Use:

- milk crates
- reclaimed timber
- rammed earth
- rusted iron
- garden beds
- floor plan marks
- long tables
- real site photos
- historical Witta and Teutoburg photos
- scans, books, archive notes, and local story fragments
- hands, tools, pots, plates, paths

Avoid:

- AI-generated brand imagery
- generic stock imagery
- abstract gradients
- decorative blobs
- over-polished hospitality imagery
- anything that hides the work underway

## Real source rule

The Harvest brand should look like the place, the people, and the history.

AI can help arrange a slide, crop a photo, draft a caption, or build a layout from supplied assets. It should not invent fake Harvest scenes, fake people, fake interiors, or fake history.

Every public image needs:

- source
- rights or permission
- consent status, if people are visible
- attribution
- story note
