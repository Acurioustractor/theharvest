# Harvest brand voice

The Harvest voice should feel like someone standing at the gate with a useful thing to say.

Plain. Local. Awake. No varnish.

## One-line identity

```text
The Harvest is a garden, kitchen, and art space taking shape in Witta.
```

## Short description

```text
The Harvest is a working community hub in Witta: a garden to grow from, a kitchen to feed from, and an art space to make in.
```

## Longer description

```text
The Harvest is a working community hub in Witta, on Jinibara Country. A former nursery is becoming three public rooms: a garden, a kitchen, and an art space. The first version is being built with local hands, old timber, milk crates, shared meals, and the people who want to help shape it.
```

## Voice rules

Use:

- short paragraphs
- named rooms
- named objects
- practical asks
- local detail
- one clear next action

Avoid:

- "we are excited to announce"
- "vibrant community hub"
- "at the heart of the community"
- "unlock", "empower", "elevate", "seamless"
- over-explaining the philosophy
- pretending the place is finished

## The Harvest sentence test

Before publishing, ask:

- Is there a room in the sentence?
- Is there a body, tool, plant, plate, or material?
- Does the line stop before it explains itself?
- Is there one clear action?

If not, rewrite.

## First lines

Good:

- The shed is starting to feel awake.
- Witta locals, this one is for you.
- We need a few hands this Saturday.
- The first table does not need to be perfect.
- The milk crates are becoming a pavilion.
- Barry's shed has more stories than walls.
- Come see what is taking shape.

Weak:

- We are excited to announce our launch.
- Community is at the heart of everything we do.
- Join us on our journey.
- The Harvest is a vibrant new destination.
- We are creating a unique multi-use precinct.

## Audience angles

### Witta locals

Lead with place, date, and invitation.

```text
The gate will be open this Saturday.

We are clearing the first garden path, marking the long table, and sorting what the shed can hold.

Come for ten minutes or two hours.
```

### Timber industry

Lead with material, tools, and continuity.

```text
The old timber is not decoration.

It is how the room remembers who built this ridge. We are using reclaimed hardwood, shed steel, and local hands to make the first version of the pavilion.
```

### Dairy industry

Lead with milk, crates, table, and daily food.

```text
The milk crates are becoming more than storage.

They are the first pavilion: stacked, carried, borrowed, returned, and built together. A small tribute to the dairy history that helped hold this district.
```

### Co-op and community enterprise

Lead with shared tools and practical ownership.

```text
The Harvest starts with shared tools, open books, and a table people can actually sit at.

The co-op question comes later. First we make the room work.
```

### Funders and landlord

Lead with proof.

```text
Phase one tests three things: can people find it, can people use it, and can the place earn enough trust to keep opening.
```

### Operators

Lead with fit.

```text
We are looking for operators who can work with the place as it is, not wait for a finished venue.
```

## Copy patterns

### Social post

```text
[Concrete first line.]

[One real detail from the week.]

[Why it matters, in one sentence.]

[One invitation.]
```

### Newsletter lead

```text
[Plain opening.]

[Site detail.]

[What changed.]

[The ask.]
```

### Event invite

```text
[Date and room.]

[What people will do.]

[What to bring.]

[How to RSVP.]
```

### Industry approach

```text
[Name the industry memory.]

[Name the object or practice we are using.]

[Invite a practical contribution.]
```

## Calls to action

Use one per piece:

- Come through this Saturday.
- Reply if you can lend a hand.
- Bring old timber, milk crates, tools, or a story.
- Tell us what this place used to be.
- Put your name down for the launch list.
- Share this with someone who knows Witta.

## Words to keep close

garden, kitchen, art space, table, shed, timber, milk, crate, tool, soil, path, pot, plate, wall, studio, nursery, hands, neighbour, Witta, ridge, working bee, pavilion, local.

## Words to keep out

precinct, destination, offering, activate, ecosystem, stakeholders, vibrant, unique, innovative, world-class, immersive, community-led if no actual community decision is named.
