# Harvest brand development guide

This guide turns the current Harvest brand memory into a repeatable AI-assisted brand development process.

It is based on the structure of `6398 - HOW-TO-USE-THESE-PROMPTS.pdf`: one prompt for discovery and research framing, one prompt for collaborative strategy building. The Harvest version is more grounded. It starts from the real place, real photos, current docs, consent rules, launch constraints, and the current public frame: Grow. Make. Gather.

Use this when you need to review, sharpen, or extend The Harvest brand across the website, deck, Notion, GHL, social, newsletter, signage, launch comms, supporter material, shop material, member material, event surfaces, or staff-facing operating tools.

## Core rule

```text
repo holds the executable memory -> Notion holds the accessible memory -> GHL publishes -> the website proves it
```

Do not create a parallel brand system. If this process proves a new rule, update `DESIGN.md`, this guide, and the Notion brand page.

## What the reference PDF teaches

The PDF is not a brand manual. It is a process guide.

Useful pieces to keep:

- two-stage workflow: discovery first, strategy second
- expert-lens framing: strategy, culture, audience, communication, experience, adoption
- research-first rule: do not make strategy from vibes only
- explicit ambiguity list before decisions
- one-page strategy summary as the final output
- short collaborative questions, one step at a time

Pieces to adapt for Harvest:

- Replace generic "market" thinking with place, provenance, rights, consent, local usefulness, and launch reality.
- Replace broad creative exploration with the public spine: Grow. Make. Gather.
- Replace invented brand imagery with approved real photos, plans, drawings, history, and site material.
- Replace "24 hour brand discovery" with a practical loop that can be run in a day but still records decisions properly.

### Stage 0: garden-opening signal

Use the launch as a public question, not a polished explanation.

Core message:

```text
The Harvest is a community garden and creative gathering place taking shape in Witta. The garden opens at the end of June.
```

Surfaces:

- `/june-20` page
- 6 public social posts
- Harvest Note / Field Note variants
- Milk Man and giant milk crate captions
- question wall
- simple wayfinding and on-site signs
- GHL "I'm coming" path

Brand rules:

- Lead with real objects: Milk Man, giant milk crate, garden, shed, pizza, questions.
- Make room for curiosity, support, doubt, and practical offers.
- Do not sound defensive about the art or the unfinished state.
- Do not over-explain the whole future model.
- One invitation per surface: come through, bring a question, bring a story, bring a pair of hands.

Acceptance checks:

- public date and time match the current page
- media is approved or clearly internal-only
- CTA points to the current RSVP / open-day path
- no members-only language leaks into public copy
- no formal co-op claim
- no invented community endorsement

### Stage 1: proof and debrief

The day after matters more than the announcement.

Core message:

```text
Here is what happened. Here is what people brought. Here is what we learned. Here is the next true step.
```

Surfaces:

- thank-you email
- photo wall
- recap article
- Notion debrief
- GHL tags / notes from attendance and paper cards
- selected social post
- website update

Brand rules:

- Use real headcount, real photos, real quotes, and real offers only.
- Publish people only where consent is clear.
- Do not announce the next event unless it is operationally real.
- If the next step is "we are reading the cards and will come back with the next opening", say that.
- Record questions and friction. Critique is signal, not brand damage.

Acceptance checks:

- every public photo has consent / rights status
- every quote is permissioned or anonymised
- the next step is true
- GHL / Notion record reflects who came, who offered, who needs follow-up
- `DESIGN.md` gets only proven decisions, not every launch impulse

### Stage 2: operating rhythm

This is where the brand becomes useful or becomes theatre.

Core message:

```text
The Harvest works because people can sell, gather, help, learn, and come back without needing a lecture.
```

Surfaces:

- maker onboarding pack
- story card with consent tick
- shelf label template
- Square catalogue naming convention
- Humanitix work-day and workshop templates
- WhatsApp crew norms
- printed operator card
- drop-off window signs
- open-hours signs

Brand rules:

- Staff surfaces must be clearer than public surfaces.
- Paper is allowed. A kraft card with the right fields beats a clever app.
- Every maker material should answer: who made this, where from, what is it, what do they get paid, can we publish their story?
- Keep the language human and operational. No brand poetry on forms people need to fill out.
- Never automate the community lane.

Acceptance checks:

- Susie / Joey can use the surface without Ben explaining it twice
- the maker knows what to do next
- the consent state is captured
- the money/till path is not confused with membership or event ticketing
- the language still feels like The Harvest, but does not slow the work down

### Stage 3: living brand system

After launch, the brand should become less speculative.

Core message:

```text
The Harvest is becoming legible through what people keep doing here.
```

Surfaces:

- updated `DESIGN.md`
- website member corner
- photo wall / member wall
- recurring event pages
- supporter deck v2
- shop story system
- seasonal field notes
- signage refinements

Brand rules:

- Promote patterns that repeated in real life.
- Retire launch-only language when it stops matching the place.
- Use the best real photos from the first month as the next visual base.
- Revisit typography, colour, and route structure only when the evidence says the current system is blocking clarity.
- Keep Grow, Make, and Gather clear even as more programs appear.

Acceptance checks:

- new brand rules are backed by observed use
- old launch copy is cleaned out
- website, Notion, GHL, and docs agree
- the ACT brand alignment map is updated before a major visual decision ships

## Surface map

Use this to decide where a brand decision lands.

| Surface | Primary job | Brand question | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website page | Prove and orient | Can a stranger understand the place and next action? | Page brief, copy, media list, CTA |
| GHL email | Move one audience | Does this person know why they got it and what to do? | Subject, preview, body, one CTA |
| Social post | Create a small public signal | Does one object, moment, or question carry the post? | Caption, media, publish gate |
| Notion page | Keep human memory accessible | Can Ben, Nic, or staff find the decision later? | Short decision record |
| Deck | Help supporters understand | Is proof carrying the story before philosophy? | 7 to 10 slide spine |
| Signage | Help bodies move | Can someone act without asking? | Sign text, location, material |
| Maker pack | Onboard a producer | Can a maker sell or contribute without confusion? | Agreement, story card, label, folder |
| Event template | Repeat a gathering | Can staff duplicate it and run it? | Humanitix template, image, reminders |
| Staff card | Protect the operating rhythm | Can someone do the job while tired? | One-page checklist |

## Minimum viable Harvest brand review

Use the smallest version that answers the job:

1. Define the surface: website, deck, social, newsletter, signage, GHL email, Notion page, supporter material, or internal guide.
2. Name the audience: locals, members, timber people, dairy people, makers, funders, operators, landlord, volunteers, or collaborators.
3. Name the public focus: Grow, Make, Gather, whole-place, garden opening, or supporting story.
4. Gather real source material: photos, plans, drawings, history, screenshots, work stories, approved assets.
5. Check constraints: rights, consent, attribution, launch status, governance claims, operational readiness.
6. Decide the job: inform, invite, ask, prove, record, sell, orient, or recruit.
7. Produce one useful output.
8. Record any decision back into the repo and Notion.

## Prompt 1: Harvest discovery and research blueprint

Paste this prompt into an LLM when you need to frame a Harvest brand review before making strategy, copy, or design decisions.

```text
Act as a Harvest brand review team:

- Place-based brand strategist
- Cultural and local-history researcher
- Audience and participation analyst
- Communications editor
- Website / CX reviewer
- Operations and adoption advisor
- Rights, consent, and source-check reviewer
- Plain-language mentor

Your job is to build a structured discovery and research blueprint for The Harvest, not creative assets.

The Harvest is a community garden and creative gathering place taking shape in Witta, on Jinibara Country.

The current public lockup is:

Witta · Jinibara Country · Garden opening end of June

The Harvest
Grow. Make. Gather.
A community garden and creative gathering place taking shape in Witta.

The public words are:

- Grow: garden opening, old nursery, seedlings, beds, soil, kids, working bees
- Make: creative build, timber, tools, signs, repair, art, workshops
- Gather: neighbours, shared tables, food, music, questions, local stories

Use these non-negotiables:

- Use real place material first: photos, plans, drawings, scans, historical images, work stories, objects, tools, timber, milk crates, rammed earth, rusted iron, garden beds, long tables.
- Do not invent Harvest imagery, fake people, fake interiors, fake history, or public proof.
- Do not imply a formal cooperative exists until governance is real.
- Do not publish sensitive stories or images without consent.
- Use Jinibara Country.
- Avoid generic cafe, wellness, venue, startup, council, or abstract community language.
- Keep Grow, Make, and Gather legible.

Input section:

Brand review goal:
[What needs to be understood, sharpened, or decided.]

Surface:
[Website / deck / Notion / GHL email / social / newsletter / signage / launch invite / supporter material / shop material / event template / staff tool / other.]

Audience:
[Who this is for.]

Focus:
[Grow / Make / Gather / whole-place / garden opening / timber / dairy / milk crates / shop / Witta history / other.]

Available inputs:
[Repo files, screenshots, photos, Notion pages, source notes, analytics, feedback, conversations, GHL emails, asset links.]

Known constraints:
[Launch status, consent limits, rights limits, operational limits, governance limits, legal or factual uncertainty, owner, deadline.]

Experience level:
[Beginner / intermediate / advanced.]

Generate your response using these sections:

1. Condensed goal
Summarise the core brand review goal in one dense statement.

2. Minimum viable review process
List the fewest steps needed to make a sound decision for this surface.

3. Ambiguity and alignment gaps
List missing or unclear information. Explain why each gap matters.

4. Working assumptions
State provisional assumptions clearly. Separate verified facts from inference.

5. Possible outcomes
Define success, partial success, and failure for this review.

6. Expert review questions
Give 5 to 7 critical questions across place, audience, voice, visuals, UX, operations, and source safety.

7. Research and source blueprint
List the research phases, exact source buckets to inspect, and the evidence needed before strategy or production.

8. Decision gates
List the checks that must pass before the output can be used publicly.

9. Where the decision lives
Name the repo file, Notion page, GHL surface, website route, or operating document that must be updated if the decision holds.

Use clear headings, short paragraphs, and practical bullets. Avoid buzzwords.
```

## Prompt 2: Harvest strategy builder

Use this after Prompt 1 and after the relevant research, screenshots, source notes, or feedback have been gathered.

```text
Act as the same Harvest brand review team:

- Place-based brand strategist
- Cultural and local-history researcher
- Audience and participation analyst
- Communications editor
- Website / CX reviewer
- Operations and adoption advisor
- Rights, consent, and source-check reviewer
- Plain-language mentor

The lead voice is a sharp Harvest editor: plain, local, useful, and allergic to generic brand language.

Your job is to turn the research and source material into a one-page Harvest brand strategy for this specific surface.

Global rules:

- Research first. Do not make strategy until the supplied evidence has been reviewed.
- One step at a time. Keep each response concise, then ask exactly one clarifying question.
- Separate verified facts, inference, and unknowns.
- No logos, fake imagery, campaign fluff, or broad creative assets unless asked.
- Strategy must be usable by someone building the actual website, deck, GHL email, social post, newsletter, sign, or Notion page.

Process:

Phase 0: Kickoff
Confirm the surface, audience, room, job, and source material.

Phase 1: Audience and participation
Define what this audience fears, wants, needs to understand, and can practically do next.

Phase 2: Place and proof
Identify which real photos, objects, rooms, materials, plans, history, or work stories should carry the surface.

Phase 3: Strategic definition
Co-write the surface strategy:

- role of the surface
- one-sentence message
- audience promise
- proof points
- voice register
- visual ingredients
- source and consent requirements
- practical call to action
- what to avoid

Final output:

Produce a one-page Harvest brand strategy summary for [surface].

Use this structure:

1. Surface
2. Audience
3. Job
4. Room / story thread
5. Core message
6. Proof to show
7. Voice rules
8. Visual rules
9. CTA
10. Guardrails
11. Open questions
12. Next production move
13. Record location
```

[launch frame, event status, food / insurance / roster gates, audience, owner]

2. Inferences
3. Unknowns

6. Copy changes

## Harvest brand review scorecard

Score each surface from 0 to 2.

| Test | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Place | Generic or placeless | Mentions Witta | Feels physically from Witta |
| Public spine | Missing | One word visible | Grow, Make, Gather relationship is clear |
| Real proof | Abstract or fake | Some real material | Real photos, plans, objects, or stories carry it |
| Voice | Corporate or vague | Mostly plain | Sounds like someone standing at the gate |
| Audience | Unclear | Broadly named | Specific person and action are obvious |
| CTA | Missing | Present but soft | One clear next action |
| Source safety | Unknown | Partly checked | Rights, consent, attribution, and facts are clear |
| Operational truth | Overpromises | Mostly accurate | Matches current launch and operating reality |
| Record | No home | Informal memory only | Decision is recorded in the right repo / Notion / GHL place |

Score guide:

- 16 to 18: ready to produce or publish
- 12 to 15: usable after targeted fixes
- 7 to 11: needs a sharper brief
- 0 to 5: restart from source material
