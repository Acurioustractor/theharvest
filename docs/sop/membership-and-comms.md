# Members: joining, welcome, emails, hearing first

**Who does this:** Ben sends the member emails. Susie and Joey reply to members in Conversations and hand-move people in the Monday sweep. Volunteers help with the Monday paper pile.

**The screen(s) you need:** GHL / LeadConnector (relationships and email) and Paper (the join form, the come-back sheet, the Monday pile). No other screen does this job.

## How someone becomes a member

1. The only door into the members list is the **join form on the `/membership` page**. Nothing else makes someone a member.
2. When someone fills that form, GHL tags them and the robot sends them one welcome email by itself. You do nothing.
3. A person can be a member and a follower and a shop maker all at once. The lists are tags, not folders. That is fine.
4. The follow form in the website footer makes a **follower**, not a member. Followers never quietly become members.

## The three lists (each has exactly one door)

1. **Newsletter (followers):** they join through the footer follow form. Their welcome fires once on its own. They get the public Field Note.
2. **Members:** they join through the `/membership` join form only. Their welcome fires once on its own. They get the Harvest Note, members first.
3. **Shop:** makers join through the `/shop` form. That is the shop list, kept separate.

## The two kinds of email (never mix them up)

1. **Welcomes are workflows.** They fire once, automatically, the moment someone joins a list. You set them up once and leave them alone. Do not touch these.
2. **Broadcasts are campaigns.** A person sits down and sends one email to one list at one time. The Field Note and the Harvest Note are broadcasts.
3. The trap: adding a tag is what fires a welcome. So a broadcast must **never** add a tag. If you tag people while sending, you can spam a whole list.

## Sending a Harvest Note to members (the safe steps)

1. Build it as a one-off **Campaign** in Marketing then Emails. Never as a workflow.
2. Set the audience to the **one** members list. Read the contact count out loud. If the number looks wrong, stop and find out why before sending.
3. Do not add or remove any tag as part of the send.
4. Set the first-name fallback to "there". (About 19 members are stored with an email where a name should be. Without the fallback the greeting renders as their email address.)
5. **Test-send to yourself first.** Open it on a phone. Click every link. Check the From address.
6. Then schedule or send.

## Members hear first

1. The people building the place hear the date before anyone. Members hear first, always.
2. The public never gets a private date (like 20 June) as a public invite. Knowing first is what membership is for. Never leak the date to make a teaser louder.
3. Invite people into the unfinished and ask for their hands, not just their attention. Plain and honest is the strategy, not garnish.

## No-auto-climb

1. Nobody moves up a rung because a robot did it. Rungs are hand-moved in the Monday sweep, earned only by a real action (helped, came back, contributed).
2. An RSVP or an event signup is never a rung, and never adds someone to the newsletter. An event yes is not a subscribe. Invite event guests to follow later, by choice.

## Never do

- Never send a broadcast to "All". One named list only.
- Never add or remove a tag while sending a broadcast.
- Never send without a test-send to yourself first.
- Never let an RSVP or event signup subscribe someone to a list.
- Never move someone up a rung automatically. Rungs are hand-moved on a real action.
- Never automate anything to the community lane (Jinibara, elders, storytellers). That is by hand, with consent, always. Ask Ben or Nic first.
- Never use a name, story, or photo of a community person without a consent check first, and it can be withdrawn anytime.
- Never put a wrong count, an unconsented photo, or an invented claim in a send. In a town of about 1,296 it costs trust you cannot rebuild. When the true thing is not ready, leave the blank.
- Voice gate before any send: no em-dashes. None of vibrant, tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, seamless, delve. Say "work days", never "working bees". Witta on Jinibara Country, colonial names in brackets.
- Susie and Joey: never send broadcasts, never edit workflows, never touch tags. Your menu hides those on purpose.

2. The price is decided after 20 June, with real members in the room. Only charge once the value is obvious (the shelf, the days, hearing first).
