# SOP: Build days, work days and volunteers

> A first-week steward should be able to run a work day from this page alone.
> We say "work days", never "working bees".

**Who does this:** Susie or Joey on the day, with Nic on the room. Ben builds the event and sends any email. A volunteer can tap names at the gate.

**The screen(s) you need:** Humanitix organiser app (the event and the gate check-in). WhatsApp (the crews). Paper (the come-back / can-help sheet on the table). GHL is only touched later, in the Monday sweep, by Ben.

A build day or work day is one repeating loop. Every event runs through the same steps below, in order.

## Steps

1. **Create the event in Humanitix.** Duplicate the "Work Day" template and change the date. Do not build from scratch. Set a cap if the room is tight (a maker session caps at 18), turn a waitlist on if it can fill, leave reminders on (Humanitix sends them itself).

2. **Invite, innermost circle first.** Crew WhatsApp first, then the members' email, then the public last (if at all). The people building the place hear first. Members hear the date before the public. Never post the date as a public invite.

3. **Word the invite as a job, not a turn-up.** Ask for hands, not attendance. "We are building the kids' corner, who is in?" beats "come along". We invite people into the unfinished and ask for their hands.

4. **Let Humanitix do the reminders.** It sends them automatically. You do nothing here.

5. **On the day:**
   - Check people in on the Humanitix organiser app. A volunteer can tap names at the gate.
   - Put the paper come-back / can-help sheet on the table. This is how someone says "I can help" or "call me back". Paper counts. A phone call counts.
   - Give everyone a small job in the first ten minutes.

6. **Close the loop the same day.**
   - Take photos, but only of people who said yes to being photographed. Share them to the crew WhatsApp group.
   - Send a one-line personal thank-you to people who came back or pitched in. A message from a person, not a template.

7. **Volunteers are a lane, not a list.** The can-help sheet goes into the Monday pile. In the Monday sweep, the person is tagged `role:volunteer` with a short skills note in GHL, and is invited into the right crew WhatsApp group. The real organising happens in the crew group. The recognition happens in person and in the after-story ("Sam and the rocket-stove crew").

8. **The roster is paper.** Maker shifts and volunteer rosters are a printed month grid on the shop wall, plus a photo of it in the right WhatsApp group. Software only when paper breaks, not before.

9. **The Monday sweep finishes the job.** One person, 30 minutes. The Saturday paper sheets get typed in, skills get tagged, and anyone who DID something (helped, came back, brought eggs) gets hand-moved up a rung. This is the only way anyone climbs. An RSVP or an attendee tag is never a rung.

## Never do

- Never move someone up a rung just because they RSVP'd or turned up. Rungs are earned by doing, and hand-moved in the Monday sweep only.
- Never auto-subscribe an attendee to the newsletter. An event yes is not a subscribe. Invite them to follow in the after-story, by choice.
- Never post a photo of a person who has not said yes. In a town of about 1,296, an uncleared photo costs trust you cannot rebuild.
- Never send broadcasts, edit workflows, or touch tags from the steward seat (Susie/Joey). That is Ben's job.
- Never automate anything to do with elders or storytellers (lane:community). That is by hand, with consent, always. Ask Ben or Nic first.
- Never invent a headcount, a build claim, or a date. If the true thing is not ready, leave the blank.
- Never use em-dashes or marketing words (vibrant, tapestry, seamless and the like) in any message. Say "work days", not "working bees". Witta is on Jinibara Country.

## When stuck

Ask Nic about the room and the day. Ask Ben about the event build, any email, or anything to do with tags, workflows, elders or storytellers.
