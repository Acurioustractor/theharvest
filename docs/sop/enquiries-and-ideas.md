# SOP: Enquiries, ideas, residencies and venue asks

> How to handle anything that comes in the front door that is not a shop EOI, a ticket, or a
> membership join. The job is simple: get back to the person, put the card in the right place,
> and never let it go cold.

**Who does this:** Susie or Joey first (replies and sorting). Ben or Nic for the calls that
need judgement (an idea worth a yes, a residency, a venue hire price).

**The screen(s) you need:** GHL / LeadConnector (the Harvest Inbox board and Conversations).
Paper for anything that came in by phone or on a sheet. Nothing else.

## The steps

1. **Find it.** Open the GHL LeadConnector app, go to the Harvest Inbox board. New enquiries,
   ideas, business and event submissions, and member questions all land here at the "New"
   stage. Anything that came in on paper or by phone goes in the Monday pile to be typed in.

2. **Work the "New" column first.** Read each card. Decide which of the four kinds it is:
   - A general question (contact form, "what are your hours", "can I visit").
   - An idea (someone says "I've got an idea" through `/get-involved?form=idea`).
   - A residency ask (someone wants to use the art space, kids' corner, or garden to make
     or run something).
   - A venue or booking ask (team day, hire the space, run their own event here).

3. **Reply, in your own words.** From Conversations, send a short human reply. Plain and warm.
   You are a person getting back to a person, not a form. The automated receipt already told
   them "we got it"; your reply is the real one.

4. **Move the card and add a next-action date.** Drag the card along the Harvest Inbox stages
   (New, In progress, Waiting on them, Resolved) as the conversation moves. Every card you
   touch gets a next-action date so nobody waits on us. Nothing sits unread past three days.

5. **Hand the judgement calls to Ben or Nic.**
   - **An idea that needs a considered yes** goes to a human to reply. Ideas are not a
     workflow and never get auto-anything. The Monday sweep types the idea into a GHL note so
     it is not lost.
   - **A residency or a maker idea (the Knock)** is a one-to-one conversation from a person,
     never a broadcast and never an automated flow. Book a real chat.
   - **A venue hire or team-day ask** gets tagged `venue-hire` or `team-day` so it is not lost.
     There is no Bookings pipeline yet (an empty board is just ceremony). When a real booking
     enquiry needs pricing, Ben or Nic handle it. [DECIDE: venue hire / team-day price and
     what is included, not set in any source doc.]

6. **If it is community, stop and ask.** If the person is a Jinibara elder, a storyteller, or
   the ask touches a community name, story or photo, do not tag them into anything, do not put
   them in a pipeline, and do not send them any automated message. Carry only `lane:community`.
   Ask Ben or Nic first, always. Consent is checked at the time of any use and can be pulled
   back anytime.

7. **Close the loop in the Monday sweep.** Paper notes get typed in. Cards with no next-action
   date get one. Ideas get reflected back to the person or noted for the after-story. Read the
   three numbers out loud (members, next-event RSVPs, makers on the shelf). The Monday sweep is
   the heartbeat. If it is skipped two weeks running, the system is quietly broken.

## Never do

- **Never auto-climb anyone.** An RSVP, a tap, or an enquiry is never a reason to move someone
  up a rung or into a pipeline. Rungs are earned by doing something real, and only a human
  moves them, by hand, in the Monday sweep.
- **Never send a broadcast to handle an enquiry**, and never add a tag while sending an email.
  Adding a tag is what fires a workflow, so a tagging broadcast can spam a whole list. One-to-one
  replies only.
- **Never touch the community lane with automation.** No flow, no list, no drip for anyone
  carrying `lane:community`, `role:elder`, or `role:storyteller`. By hand, with consent, always.
  Elder authority is a veto, not a preference.
- **Never invent a price, a date, a count, or a name.** In a town of about 1,296 a wrong figure
  or an unconsented photo costs trust you cannot rebuild. When the true thing is not ready,
  leave the blank rather than fill it.
- **Never make a new tag from your head.** Tags come only from the canonical tag map. A new tag
  means the map gets updated first.
- **Never open Settings, Marketing, or Automation in GHL.** Stewards live in Conversations,
  Opportunities, Calendars and Contacts. Broadcasts and workflows are Ben's job.

## The voice gate (before any reply you send)

- No em-dashes. Use commas, periods, brackets.
- None of these words: vibrant, tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, seamless,
  delve.
- "Work days", never "working bees".
- Place names: Witta on Jinibara Country, colonial names in brackets where needed.

## When stuck

Ask Ben or Nic. Anything that needs a price, a residency yes, a venue decision, or touches a
community name, story or photo is theirs to decide, not yours to guess.
