# SOP: Photos, stories and consent

A simple, repeatable way to take, store, and use photos and stories at The Harvest without ever using someone's face or words without their yes.

**Who does this:** stewards on the floor (Susie, Joey) and volunteers on event days. Ben or Nic handle anything in the community lane (Jinibara, elders, storytellers).

**The screen(s) you need:** GHL/LeadConnector app (the consent note on the person's record), WhatsApp (sending the photo to the right crew group), and Paper (the maker's story card and the kraft drop-off label). Square and Humanitix are not used for consent.

---

## The one rule

No photo of a person, and no story in someone's words, goes anywhere (the shelf, the website, an email, a WhatsApp group, social) until that person has said yes. Consent is checked at the time of each use, and a person can change their mind anytime. If you do not have a clear yes, leave the blank.

---

## Steps: a maker's photo and story

1. When you onboard a maker, give them the kraft folder with their story card.
2. On the story card they write who they are, what they make, and where, and they tick the photo consent box.
3. Scan or photograph the signed story card into the maker's Drive folder (`Shop/Makers/<name>/`).
4. Open the maker's contact in the GHL app and add a short consent note (for example: "photo consent yes, story card on file, dated the day they signed").
5. Only then use their photo on the shelf label, the website, or an email.
6. If the maker has not ticked the box, use their product name and origin only. No face, no quote.

## Steps: the first-shelf photo

1. When a maker's item goes on the shelf the first time, take a photo of that shelf placement.
2. Send it that same day to the maker, as a one-line WhatsApp message from you, not an automated send.
3. This is staff work, by hand. It is one of the things we never automate.

## Steps: photos on an event or work day

1. Before you photograph people, tell them what the photo is for (the crew WhatsApp group, the website photo wall, or an email recap).
2. Only photograph people who are happy to be in it. A person-led photo is cleared first, every time.
3. After the day, send the cleared photos to the relevant crew WhatsApp group (garden, art space, kitchen/events, or makers).
4. Photos that go on our website photo wall or member wall are consent-checked first. [DECIDE: where and how an event guest's photo consent is recorded, since the story-card tick only covers makers]
5. The Monday sweep types any paper notes in. Photos themselves are not a rung and never move anyone up a journey.

## Steps: anything in the community lane (Jinibara, elders, storytellers)

1. Stop. This is never your call as a steward, and it is never automated.
2. Ask Ben or Nic first, always.
3. Any yarn, photo, or transcript captured with community enters the owes-ledger and is held under their consent.
4. Consent here is checked at the time of any use and is revocable anytime. Elder authority is a veto, not a preference.

## The voice gate (before any caption, label, or email)

- No em-dashes. Use commas, periods, or brackets.
- Do not use: vibrant, tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, seamless, delve.
- Say "work days", never "working bees".
- Place names: Witta on Jinibara Country, colonial names in brackets.
- No invented counts, dates, names, or attendance. If the true thing is not ready, leave the blank.

---

> ### Never do
> - Never use a person's face or words without a clear yes.
> - Never automate anything in the community lane (Jinibara, elders, storytellers). By hand, with consent, always.
> - Never make the first-shelf photo an automated send. It is a message from a person.
> - Never invent a count, a date, a name, or a build claim. In a town of about 1,296, a wrong figure or an unconsented photo costs trust you cannot rebuild.
> - Never expose any individual maker's pricing or margin. The signage names the maker and origin, with the shop-wide pledge doing the price talking.
> - Never treat an RSVP or an attendee tag as permission to use someone's photo, and never as a journey rung.

---

**When stuck:** ask Ben or Nic. Anything to do with elders, storytellers, or Jinibara goes to them first, every time.
