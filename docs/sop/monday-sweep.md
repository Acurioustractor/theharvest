# The Monday sweep (the heartbeat)

About 30 minutes, one person. This is the heartbeat of the whole system. The robots only ever say "we got it" and "don't forget". The sweep is where a human reads what came in over the week and sorts it by hand. If the sweep gets skipped two weeks running, the system is quietly broken. That is the warning sign to watch for.

**Who does this:** one person, and it rotates. Ben starts it off, then it passes around.

**The screen(s) you need:** GHL (the LeadConnector app or GHL on a computer) and Paper (Saturday's come-back sheets and any drop-off labels). That is it for the sweep itself.

## The steps

1. **Open the Harvest Inbox pipeline.** Look at the "New" column. For each card, either answer it now, or give it a next-action date (a task with a date on it). Nothing in "New" should be left with no plan.

2. **Open Conversations.** Find anything unread and reply to it. Nothing should sit unread for more than 3 days.

3. **Open the Shop board.** Go through the cards. Any card without a next-action date gets one. That is the rule, so no maker goes cold waiting on us.

4. **The paper pile.** Take Saturday's come-back sheets and any kraft drop-off labels and type them in. Add tags from the tag map only (never make up a new tag). If someone said they want to join a crew, note the crew so it gets a `pod:` invite later.

5. **New shop EOIs.** Check the receipt fired (the maker got their "we got it" message). For the keen ones, book a chat with them.

6. **Who DID something this week.** Anyone who helped, came back, or contributed (brought eggs, lent a hand): hand-move their Membership Journey rung up by one. This is the only way anyone climbs. An RSVP or a turning-up is not a rung. Doing something real is.

7. **Read three numbers out loud:** members, RSVPs for the next event, and makers on the shelf. If a number surprises you, find out why before you do anything else.

When you type someone in, tags come from the tag map only. A new tag means the map gets updated first. If a count ever looks wrong, stop and find out why.

## Never do (in the sweep)

- Never move anyone up a rung just because they RSVP'd or showed up. A rung is earned by doing.
- Never send a broadcast or an email blast as part of the sweep. The sweep sorts and reads, it does not send.
- Never add a tag while sending anything (adding a tag is what fires a welcome, so it can spam a whole list).
- Never make up a new tag on the spot. Update the tag map first, then use it.
- Never touch the community lane with anything automatic. Anyone tagged `lane:community`, `role:elder`, or `role:storyteller` (Jinibara people, elders, storytellers) is worked by hand, with consent, and you ask Ben or Nic first. Always.
- Never do The Knock (a 1:1 maker ask) or the after-day photo message as part of the sweep. Those come from a person, by hand.

## When stuck

Ask Ben. Anything to do with elders, storytellers, or the community lane, ask Ben or Nic before you do a single thing.
