# Gatherings and open days: the run pattern

**Who does this:** Susie or Joey, with Nic on the day and a volunteer on the gate. Ben sets up the broadcasts.

**The screen(s) you need:** Humanitix organiser app (the event and the gate), WhatsApp (the crews), Paper (the come-back sheet on the table). GHL only later, in the Monday sweep. The till is Square if anything sells on the day.

A gathering or open day is the same five steps every time. Make the event, invite people in the right order, let the reminders run, run the day with a job for everyone, then close the loop after. Same steps for a work day, a workshop, or a market day.

## The steps

1. **Make the event in Humanitix.** Do not build it from scratch. Open the "Work Day" or "Workshop" template, duplicate it, and change the date. That is the whole job. Set a seat cap if the room is tight (the cap is the number the room actually holds [DECIDE: confirm the seat cap for each space]). Add a waitlist if it might fill. Free or paid is fine; Humanitix handles the GST.

2. **Invite people, innermost circle first.** The order matters and it never changes. Crew WhatsApp groups first. Members next, by email. The public last, if at all. The people building the place hear before anyone else. That is what membership is for. Never put a public invite out for a members-first date.

3. **Write the invite as an ask for hands, not attendance.** "We're building the kids' corner, who's in?" beats "come along". We invite people into something unfinished. Plain and honest, never marketing. The member email is a broadcast (Ben sends it from the members list). The crew ask is just a WhatsApp message.

4. **Let the reminders run.** Humanitix sends the reminder and the day-before nudge by itself. You do not send these by hand. Do not add any tag to anyone as part of inviting them. An RSVP is not a subscribe, and a tap is not a lead.

5. **Run the day.** Check people in on the Humanitix organiser app on the gate phone, a volunteer taps names. Put the paper come-back / can-help sheet on the table, that is the real relationship instrument, not admin. Give everyone a small job in the first ten minutes. If anything sells, it goes through Square (the till), tickets were already through Humanitix.

6. **Close the loop after the day, by hand.** Take photos, but only of people who said yes. Send them to the crew WhatsApp group. Send one short personal thank-you to people who came, a message from a person, never a template. This is staff work, not a workflow. The thank-you email to members goes after the day has happened and carries one true thing learned (Ben sends it).

7. **Hand the paper in on Monday.** Bring the come-back / can-help sheet to the Monday sweep. Someone types the names in, adds skills notes, and hand-moves anyone who really did something (helped, came back, brought eggs) up a rung. The attendee headcount tag is never a rung on its own. If a Jinibara elder or storyteller came, that is by hand with Ben or Nic, never a list, consent first.

## Never do

- Never send a public invite for a date members are meant to hear first.
- Never add a tag to anyone while inviting or broadcasting (adding a tag fires an automated email and can spam a whole list).
- Never treat an RSVP or a check-in as a newsletter subscribe, or as a rung up the ladder. Invite event guests to follow later, by choice, in the after-story.
- Never use a community-lane person (elder, storyteller, Jinibara) in any automation, list, or drip. By hand, with consent, always. Ask Ben or Nic first.
- Never post a photo of a person who has not said yes.
- Never invent a headcount, a date, attendance, or a build claim. In a town of about 1,296, a wrong figure or an unconsented photo costs trust you cannot rebuild. If the true thing is not ready, leave the blank.
- Voice gate before any send: no em-dashes. None of these words: vibrant, tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, seamless, delve. Say "work days", never "working bees". Place names are Witta on Jinibara Country, colonial names in brackets.

## When stuck

Ask Ben (broadcasts, workflows, the Humanitix setup) or Nic (the day, the gate, the crews). For anything touching an elder or a storyteller, ask Ben or Nic first, always.
