# Taking money: which till for what

> A first-week steward only needs to know which till a thing goes through. Not how the money plumbing works. This sheet tells you that.

## Who does this

Stewards on open hours (Susie and Joey anchor it, plus any maker or volunteer on a shift).

## The screen(s) you need

Three tills, never mixed up: **Square POS** (the till for shelves), **Humanitix organiser** (tickets), and a **GHL link** (belonging, only when membership becomes paid). Plus **Paper** for anything written by hand. Xero is the ACT side and is not a steward screen.

## The one rule

- Things on shelves go through **Square**.
- Tickets go through **Humanitix**.
- Belonging or membership goes through the **GHL link** (GHL plus Stripe when it is a paid tier).

That is the whole decision. Pick the till by what the thing is.

## Steps: a shop sale

1. Customer brings an item from the shelf to you.
2. Ring it up in **Square POS**. Each item is listed under its maker, so the sale records against the right person.
3. Take the payment on the Square Reader (tap or chip).
4. Hand over the item. The receipt is automatic.
5. If this is the first time that maker's product has sold, send the maker a one-line WhatsApp that day with a photo of the shelf. That close-the-loop is your job, not the software's.

You do not work out the split at the till. Square records the sale; the payout happens later (see below).

## Steps: an event or workshop ticket

1. Tickets are sold in **Humanitix**, not at the shelf and not in Square.
2. If someone pays for a ticket on the day, take it through the Humanitix event, not Square.
3. At the gate, open the **Humanitix organiser app** and tap names to check people in.
4. Free events still run through Humanitix. They cost nothing to set up.

## Steps: membership money

1. Right now membership is a free tagged list. There is usually nothing to take.
2. When a paid membership tier exists, it is collected through the **GHL link** (GHL plus Stripe). [DECIDE: the membership tier price, decided after 20 June with real members in the room]
3. A human confirms the relationship first. Stripe only collects after that conversation. Never charge someone into membership from a form or an RSVP.

## How makers get paid (not at the till)

- The maker keeps ownership of the item until it sells. They are only paid once it sells.
- The maker keeps about **75 to 80 percent** of the sale; the shop keeps **20 to 25 percent** to cover card fees, staffing, and overhead. [DECIDE: the exact split inside that band, confirmed by Standard Ledger before the pledge percent is printed]
- Payouts are run from the Square sales report, by bank transfer, on a set rhythm (suggested Monday morning looking back at the weekend). [DECIDE: whether to add a month-end run on top of the weekly one] At launch this is done by hand each week (Path A). We only add a paid consignment tool if makers grow past about 20 or reconciliation starts taking more than an hour a week. That gets reviewed at six months.
- A steward does not need to do the payout maths at the till. The till just records the sale.

## Never do

- **Never use GHL to sell anything or to pay a maker.** GHL is the maker relationship record only. The till is Square.
- **Never run a shop sale through Humanitix, or a ticket through Square.** Wrong till, broken records.
- **Never charge anyone for a membership rung, or move them up a rung, from an RSVP or a tap.** Rungs are earned by doing, and hand-moved later.
- **Never tell a maker, or write on a sign, what any single maker is paid in dollars.** The shop-wide pledge ("makers keep about X percent") goes on the wall; the per-product label names the maker and origin only, never their pricing or margin.
- **Never take money for anything in the community lane** (a Jinibara, elder, or storyteller matter). That lane runs on consent and is worked by hand, never through a till.
- **Never invent a price, a split, a payout figure, or a maker name.** If you do not know it, leave it blank and ask. In a small town a wrong figure costs trust you cannot rebuild.
- **Never add a new app or screen** to take money. If one of the three tills will not do the job, that is a decision for Ben or Nic, not a workaround.

## When stuck

Ask Susie or Joey first. If it is about a split, a payout, or which entity the money belongs to, that goes to Ben or Nic (Standard Ledger sits behind them on the numbers). When in doubt, take nothing and write it on the come-back sheet for the Monday sweep.
