# The community talking surface: WhatsApp Community + crews

**Who does this:** the crew admins (one named person per group). Setup and the Monday tidy-up are Ben or Nic. Susie and Joey help people join in person.

**The screen(s) you need:** WhatsApp (the crews). GHL/LeadConnector only for the Monday sweep, to add the matching tag. That is it. WhatsApp is one of the four operator screens plus paper, and this is its whole job.

---

## What this is

The Harvest talks to itself on a WhatsApp Community named The Harvest. It has one announcement channel up top, plus a crew group underneath for each crew: garden, art space, kitchen/events, and makers. One human admin runs each group.

Chat organises the work. It never decides the work. Deciding happens on a work day, at the long table, with the chalkboard and the come-back sheet. The chat is for "who can lend a marquee", not for "should we do this".

We are not adding a community platform or app. WhatsApp is a sixth-grade-simple surface everyone already has. Nobody learns anything new.

## Steps to set up a crew group

1. Wait until after the 20 June day. Name the crews from the real energy you saw, not before.
2. In WhatsApp, make one group for the crew (for example, Garden). One group per crew, no more.
3. Pin a short two-line message at the top: what this group is for, and "photos of people only with their okay".
4. Pick one human admin for that group. Write down who it is. One admin per group, always.
5. Tell Ben or Nic the group exists, so the matching GHL tag (for example `pod:garden`) gets added in the next Monday sweep.

## Steps to let someone join a crew

1. A person turns up, helps out, and you can see they belong in that crew.
2. The admin adds them to the WhatsApp group by hand.
3. There is no public join link. The door is a person, every time.
4. In the next Monday sweep, the person gets the matching `pod:` tag in GHL. The tag mirrors the group. The group never depends on the tag, so if the tag is missing the group still works.

## Steps to run the announcement channel

1. Only admins post in the announcement channel. It is for: gate times, what is new on the shelf, and one ask that needs many hands (for example "who can lend trestles").
2. Keep one ask to the channel, then let the replies happen down in the crew groups.
3. Members hear things first. Do not leak the 20 June date to the public as a teaser, and do not post anything to the channel that should stay members-first.

## Steps when a bigger idea shows up in chat

1. If someone has a bigger idea that needs a real, considered yes, do not try to decide it in the chat.
2. Send them to the form at `/get-involved?form=idea`. That lands in GHL as a `community-idea` and a human replies.
3. Real deciding waits for the work day. The chat organises, it never governs.

## Steps for photos and the after-day message

1. Before any photo of a person goes in a group, get that person's okay. In a town of about 1,296 people, an unconsented photo costs trust you cannot win back.
2. The after-day photo message to a maker or a guest is sent by a person, by hand. It is never automated. If something sold, tell the maker that day: one line with a photo of the first shelf placement.

## What the Monday sweep does with the chat

1. Once a week, one person spends about 30 minutes on the sweep. This is the heartbeat. If it is skipped two weeks running, the system is quietly broken.
2. In the sweep: add any new `pod:` tags so GHL mirrors the groups, and type any decisions or ideas from the chat into GHL notes.
3. The sweep also reflects ideas back to people in the after-story email. The chat has no memory of its own, so the sweep is the memory.

---

> ## Never do
>
> - Never use the chat to decide things. It organises, it never governs. Deciding happens at the work day.
> - Never post a public join link. The join door is always a human.
> - Never run a group with more than one admin, or a crew with more than one group.
> - Never post a photo of a person without their okay first.
> - Never send anything automated into the community lane (`lane:community`, `role:elder`, `role:storyteller`). Those people are worked by hand, with consent, always. Elder authority is a veto, not a preference.
> - Never let a `pod:` tag control the group. The tag mirrors the group, never the other way around.
> - Never use em-dashes. Use commas, periods, or brackets. Avoid these words: vibrant, tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, seamless, delve. Say "work days", never "working bees".
> - Never invent a count, a date, an attendance number, or a build claim. If the true thing is not ready, leave the blank.
> - Never add a fifth tool. The system is four screens plus paper. WhatsApp only earns a replacement if it demonstrably breaks (no threads, ideas getting lost, mess across three or more groups). Even then, reopening is Ben's call on a named pain, not on ambition.

**When stuck:** ask Ben or Nic. For anything touching elders, storytellers, or community names, photos, or stories, ask Ben or Nic first, always, before you act.
