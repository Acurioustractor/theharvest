## Design Principles (from conversation)

These should inform the Vision page tone:

1. **Gallery model** — "Someone will come, put an exhibition on, love it, then it'll die, and the next person comes in"
2. **Unfinished canvas** — "You're not coming to something that's finished. You're coming to something you can be a part of"
3. **Test and iterate** — "We could spend $200K on a restaurant and no one comes. Can we test little pop-ups?"
4. **Nature first** — "We really want to bring people out to nature, picking rocket from the garden"
5. **Community contributions** — "Everyone brings one chair. The cutlery is all random spoons and forks. Everything here has been an offering."
6. **Alice in Wonderland** — "All these different experiences you stumble into... maybe you're going for one thing and you're surprised to see something else"
