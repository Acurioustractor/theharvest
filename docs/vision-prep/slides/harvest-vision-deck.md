# Our Design Philosophy

Three principles guide everything we build:

**🎨 Gallery, Not Museum**
Nothing is super permanent. Like an art gallery — exhibitions come, people love them, they evolve, new ones take their place. The space is always becoming.

**🔥 Test Before You Build**
Pop-ups before permanent builds. We test with oysters and pizza before investing in a restaurant. Every dollar follows proof of demand.

**🖌️ Unfinished Canvas**
You're not coming to something that's finished — you're coming to something you can be a part of. Every chair, every plant, every event is a community contribution.

# The Oyster Story

Sean is an Aboriginal oyster farmer from Stradbroke Island.

When colonizers arrived in Brisbane, they **blew up the Aboriginal oyster leases** for limestone — the Treasury building, the banks, the city foundations are built from his ancestors' oyster shells.

**At The Harvest:**
- Sean sells oysters direct to community (better margin than fish markets)
- People eat on picnic blankets on the lawn
- Shells are collected and used by Thais to make **rammed earth flooring**
- The oyster shells return to the earth — full cycle

> "From seedling to harvest to compost — and back again."

---

# Year 2: Integrate & Scale (2027)

*"Connect the ecosystem"*

- Main building activated: modular restaurant + event space
- ACT Farm → Harvest supply chain established
- Artist & maker residency programs
- Youth vocational training partnerships
- Community operators identified & in training

---

# Year 3: Hand Over the Keys (2028)

*"Community takes the lead"*

- Community cooperative takes operational lead
- ACT shifts to capacity support only
- Model documented for replication
- Full ecosystem integration with ACT Farm

> Our success is measured by our irrelevance. We build to hand over, not to hold.
