Summary

### Restaurant and Dining Space Design

- Main dining area to feature a raised timber floor to create movement and visual interest
- Proposed eating area with raised planter boxes, tables, and lounge-style seating
- Fireplace concept discussed as a central feature, potentially positioned against a wall or as a freestanding element
- Bar area with large window into kitchen to create visual connection
- High table seating along walls for bar-style dining
- Strong emphasis on creating a "homefeel" atmosphere with couches, table lamps, and residential-style furniture
- Consideration of seasonal programming with four shows per year (summer, winter, autumn, spring) to encourage repeat visitors
- Potential mezzanine level above bar area for additional seating and art viewing

### Kitchen and Service Areas

- Kitchen layout with window for customer viewing
- Service corridor behind bar to prevent kitchen staff from crossing dining areas
- Long service area with storage shelves accessible from multiple points
- Service hallway to bathroom areas to prevent direct sightlines
- Cold room and storage near fridge area
- Discussion about keeping kitchen functionality while maintaining flexibility
- Need to confirm trolley access and service flow with kitchen staff

### Art Gallery and Display Features

- Entire wall to function as art gallery with flexible hanging systems
- Proposed rail system for quick gallery changes
- Inspiration from Design Week Sydney exhibition with art hanging at various heights including from ceiling
- Wire system from ceiling to floor for sliding art displays
- Consideration of library-style rolling ladders for accessing high art installations
- Metal frames and pressed metal elements as design features [noted in user notes]
- Dark painted walls (potentially lime wash) to create gallery atmosphere
- Frames for historical information about the place

### Workshop and Maker Space

- Artist residency studio concept with workspace for creating exhibition pieces
- Production facility where artists work on sets and pieces that will be displayed
- Potential for visitors to walk through workshop as part of experience, like "stepping into Narnia"
- Storage and work bench requirements
- Multi-medium approach with different artists (designers, chefs, sound designers, lighting designers) rotating through residencies

### Indigenous Design Integration

- Discussion with Regional Arts Australia funding organization
- Strong emphasis on incorporating First Nations design principles and circular/round forms rather than colonial square shapes
- Proposal for First Nations designer in residence to help with gallery and founding principles
- Reference to Krakani Lumi cabin in Tasmania as inspiration - timber cave design by First Nations designer
- Connection to local Aboriginal stories and Wisman Museum history
- Materials (earth, metal, timber) tied to Indigenous storytelling

### Materials and Aesthetic

- Rust metal and pressed metal as key material elements [noted in user notes]
- Rammed earth wall with sliding rust metal gate as feature element
- Timber flooring with visible grain, avoiding overly dark Melbourne aesthetic
- Pressed metal ceiling treatments
- Mix of earthy materials: rust, metal, timber, earth
- Stained plywood cladding with strips covering edges
- Acoustic panels in fabric or paper for sound management
- Potential use of cedar from Barry's property for interior applications

### Flexibility and Multi-Use Concepts

- Everything on wheels or dropped into floor for easy reconfiguration
- Seasonal bump-outs where space can be completely transformed
- Restaurant setup as flexible as possible, not permanent
- Stage area that can serve multiple functions
- Collapsible blocks and modular elements for quick space transformation
- Two-show system with A-side/B-side setup for quick changeovers

### Ceiling and Lighting Features

- Drop ceiling panels in some areas for acoustic treatment and lighting diffusion
- Hanging elements from ceiling for ambience and flexibility
- Retractable roof panels for hanging installations and soundproofing
- Central hanging features for performance lighting

### Outdoor Connections

- Garden area with raised planter boxes and seating around windows
- Courtyard spaces for both restaurant and studio use
- Beer garden atmosphere at back for restaurant patrons
- Window converted to planter box feature
- Potential ceremony starting in pavilion with drinks, then moving down stairs through garden
- Interactive experience where guests pick produce from garden before meal

### Water Features and Sustainability

- Proposed water feature with walls of water pouring into river channel
- Raised floor (100mm) to accommodate waterproofed channels
- Water flowing through mesh or chains for softened effect
- Potential pond feature with fragrance elements
- All rainwater captured and stored on-site
- Solar power generation on-site
- Sewer treatment on-site
- Fire in upper garden wrapped in copper to generate hot water
- Zero-waste approach with composting and material reuse

### Performance and Stage Elements
