## How this space works

A quick orientation. Everything lives in spaces, the menu down the left. Events are in What's On. You can use The Harvest in your browser, or get the Mighty Networks app on your phone and find The Harvest. Booking and paying for events happens right here in the space, never on the day.

## Find your way around

**What's On.** Every gathering, work day, class, and shared meal. Book the paid ones, RSVP to the free ones. Start with this weekend.

**Garden Crew.** For the people who come to grow. Work days, what is ready to pick, and what the beds need.

**The Shop Makers.** For the people who make. What the shared shelf might stock, and how to get your produce or your work onto it.

**Offerings.** Got an idea for a class, a talk, music, a kids activity, or a skill to share? Suggest it here. Much of what we run next will start in this space.

**Community Notice Board.** What is coming up, surplus produce to share, and messages from the community. The digital version of the board we build on Friday.

**Ask a Steward.** Questions, or something you need a hand with. Ask here and one of us will answer. Nothing is too small. If you ever get stuck getting in, this is the place.

**Members and Leaderboard.** See who else is part of this. The Harvest is its people first.

## How membership works

Joining is free. This weekend you pay only for what you come to, the prices are above. An ongoing membership is coming soon at $30 a week, with every event included and member-only spaces. For now, just join, come along, and see what you think.

Whatever you turn up for, you are a member.
