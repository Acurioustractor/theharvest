## Operating rules

1. **GHL publishes.** Empathy Ledger holds story + media. Notion records what happened. Obsidian is the thinking layer. WhatsApp is raw source. This is the existing system — we are fitting into it, not replacing it.
2. **Member list first, always.** Every email-led touchpoint lands in member inboxes before any social or public mention.
3. **One touchpoint per week, two max.** The "less, clearly" principle from the Notion alignment page applies to comms as much as to operations.
4. **No fabrication.** Never claim attendance figures, completed builds, or material origins that aren't verified. See [memory: no-fabricated-facts](../../).
5. **Reply-led RSVP only.** No public form for 20 June. Replies to the email are the headcount source.
6. **Permission first.** Person-led stories stay draft until consent is explicit. See [harvest-blog-empathy-ledger-flow.md §Approval Rule](harvest-blog-empathy-ledger-flow.md).

## Repeatable patterns (post-launch)

After 20 June, the cadence collapses back to monthly. Use the same five-touchpoint shape for each subsequent event:

| Touchpoint | When | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Tease | T-4 weeks | Optional, member list only |
| Invite | T-3 weeks | Member list (and broader as comfort grows) |
| Nudge | T-2 weeks | Member list, only if reply count is thin |
| Practical | T-2 days | Member list, those holding a seat |
| Thank-you + photos | T+3 days | Member list, then social cross-post |

Plus the monthly **Harvest Note** — the one that's promised in the member welcome email at [welcome-email-and-ghl-workflow.md §Workflow 1](welcome-email-and-ghl-workflow.md). That's the steady-state list message. One letter from Ben or Nic. What changed in the garden. What's coming. One honest question. One small ask. Same shape every time.

Until the first one goes out, the member welcome is partly aspirational. The first Harvest Note should land **in July**, after the 20 June debrief, as the first proof that the promised cadence is real.

## Voice + style enforcement

Every send goes through these checks before scheduling:

- [ ] No em-dashes (`grep -- "—"` on the draft)
- [ ] No AI vocab (vibrant, tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial)
- [ ] No "we're excited to", "we're thrilled", "embark on a journey"
- [ ] No "working bees" (use "work days" or "Wednesday crew")
- [ ] No fabricated counts, dates, or material origins
- [ ] Jinibara Country named explicitly
- [ ] Witta named explicitly (not "the hinterland" as a substitute)
- [ ] Reply-to address: hello@theharvestwitta.com.au
- [ ] If photos of people, permission checked or photos shot without faces

These checks live in [harvest-brand-voice.md](../brand/harvest-brand-voice.md) — this list is the inline reminder.
