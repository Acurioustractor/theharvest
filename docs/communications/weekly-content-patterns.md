# Weekly Content Patterns

This is the simple weekly engine.

Use GHL as the publishing desk. Use Notion as the record. Use Premiere for videos that need shape.

For the weekly talking loop, use:

```text
docs/communications/weekend-debrief-to-content.md
```

## The Weekly Question

Every week, answer this:

```text
What are we learning about the place, and what do we need people to do next?
```

That gives the content enough direction without turning it into a marketing machine.

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday: Choose The Week

Pick one weekly theme:

- place is waking up
- garden is forming
- kitchen is possible
- art space is becoming real
- locals can help
- event is coming
- thank you and next step

Then pick one practical ask:

- comment with ideas
- send a message
- join the list
- offer materials
- come to a working bee
- share a memory
- tag someone local

### Tuesday: Place Note

Job: make people feel the site.

Best format:

- photo post
- 15 second reel
- short caption

Pattern:

```text
[Specific thing we saw.]

[What it made us notice.]

[What it might become.]

[Question.]
```

### Thursday: Practical Ask

Job: make it easy for locals to help.

Best format:

- Facebook first
- can also go to Instagram
- clear list

Pattern:

```text
Quick local ask.

We are looking for:

- [thing]
- [thing]
- [person with skill]

If you have one, or know someone who does, send us a message.
```

### Weekend: Human Or Progress Moment

Job: show movement.

Best format:

- reel
- casual photo dump
- thank you post

Pattern:

```text
Small progress this week.

[What happened.]

[Who helped or what changed.]

[What is next.]
```

## Four Repeatable Content Pillars

### 1. Place

Use when the site itself is the story.

Examples:

- light through the shed
- the old nursery rows
- empty kitchen space
- long table test
- milk crate art
- tools, timber, doors, paths

Question:

```text
What could this hold?
```

### 2. People

Use when trust is the story.

Examples:

- Barry and the nursery
- Nic shaping the space
- Susie and Joey as stewards
- local growers
- working bee helpers
- artists and makers

Question:

```text
Who else should be part of this?
```

### 3. Making

Use when action is the story.

Examples:

- moving tables
- clearing paths
- testing a kitchen setup
- planting
- painting
- fixing
- hanging lights

Question:

```text
What should we make here first?
```

### 4. Invitation

Use when the audience needs a next step.

Examples:

- join the list
- come to a working bee
- bring tools
- offer plants
- share photos
- volunteer

Question:

```text
Want to help?
```

## Test Posts

### Post 1: The Place Is Not Finished

Use: Instagram and Facebook

Format: photo or 15 second reel

Caption:

```text
The Harvest is not arriving finished.

That might be the best thing about it.

There is room here for a garden that people shape by showing up. A kitchen that starts with simple food and a long table. An art space that can be useful before it is polished.

This place will tell us what it wants to become, but only if enough people walk through it.

What should we test first?

#TheHarvestWitta #Witta #Maleny #SunshineCoastHinterland #CommunityBuilding
```

Video brief:

```text
Length: 15 seconds
Template: Place Moment
Clips: wide shed, garden path, detail, empty table space
Overlay: What should we test first?
```

### Post 2: Local Materials Callout

Use: Facebook first

Format: photo post

Caption:

```text
Quick local ask.

We are starting to collect useful things for the first version of The Harvest.

Not fancy. Useful.

We are looking for:

- sturdy outdoor chairs
- folding tables
- timber that still has life in it
- garden tools
- large pots
- shade cloth
- people who know plants
- people who know kitchens

If you have something sitting around that could help, send us a message.

The first version of this place will be built from what is already here and what the community brings.
```

Video brief:

```text
Length: 20 to 30 seconds
Template: Story
Clips: empty space, close-ups of tools/materials, someone carrying or sorting
Overlay: Useful things wanted
```

### Post 3: Garden First

Use: Instagram and Facebook

Format: reel

Caption:

```text
The garden is probably the first honest thing here.

Before the website, before the posters, before the big plans, there were plants, shade, paths, soil, and people who cared for the nursery.

Now we are working out what the next layer should be.

Food beds. Native propagation. Compost. Kids digging. Working bees. Quiet mornings with people who know more than us.

If you live nearby, what would you grow first?

#TheHarvestWitta #CommunityGarden #NativeNursery #Witta #Maleny
```

Video brief:

```text
Length: 30 seconds
Template: Story
Clips: nursery rows, leaves, hands in soil, path, wide garden
Overlay: What should we grow first?
```

### Post 4: Kitchen As A Long Table

Use: Instagram and Facebook

Format: 30 second reel

Caption:

```text
The kitchen does not need to start as a restaurant.

It can start as a long table.

Coffee after a working bee. Soup from someone local. A producer telling the story of what they grow. A simple meal after a workshop.

That feels like the right first step.

What would make you sit down at the table?

#TheHarvestWitta #HinterlandFood #Witta #Maleny #LocalProducers
```

Video brief:

```text
Length: 30 seconds
Template: Story
Clips: kitchen doorway, bench, table, cup, garden produce, people setting up
Overlay: Start with a long table
```

### Post 5: Art Space Without The Fancy Language

Use: Instagram and Facebook

Format: photo or reel

Caption:

```text
The art space does not need fancy language.

It needs tables, tools, light, storage, people making things, and a reason to come back.

Workshops. Repair. Drawing. Music. Objects. Experiments. Kids making the kids area stranger and better than adults would.

That is enough to begin.

What would you make here?

#TheHarvestWitta #Makerspace #ArtistResidency #Witta #Maleny
```

Video brief:

```text
Length: 15 to 30 seconds
Template: Place Moment or Story
Clips: room detail, light, wall, table, hands, object
Overlay: What would you make here?
```

### Post 6: End Of Week Progress

Use: Facebook and Instagram

Format: reel or photo dump

Caption:

```text
Small progress this week.

[Add what actually happened.]

The good bit is that every small job makes the place easier to understand.

One corner clears. One path opens. One person offers a thing we needed. One conversation changes the next step.

That is probably how this place gets built.

Thanks to everyone already leaning in.
```

Video brief:

```text
Length: 45 seconds
Template: Walkthrough
Clips: before shot, action, detail, after shot, human moment
Overlay: Small progress this week
```

## Video Capture List

Shoot these whenever you are on site:

- 5 second wide shot of each zone
- 5 second close-up of one detail
- 5 second walking shot through a doorway or path
- 5 second hands shot
- 5 second object shot
- 5 second sound moment
- one sentence voice note: "What changed this week?"

That is enough for a reel.

## Weekly Review

Every Monday, look at:

- which post got comments
- which post got practical offers
- which post drove messages
- which video held attention
- which ask was ignored

Then adjust the next week.

Do not overthink the metrics. At this stage, comments and useful replies matter more than reach.
