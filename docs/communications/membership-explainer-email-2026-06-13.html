Membership, made simple.
Grow Make Gather

Membership at The Harvest is free. It is not a fee and not a discount card.

Being a member means three simple things. You hear about what is on before anyone else. You find the next useful thing to be part of. And you get a say in how this place grows.

Some people come for the garden. Some make for the shop. Some come for the workshops. All of them are members, and all of them help shape what happens next.

How to be part of it

The best way in is to show up. Come to a work day. Make something for the shop. Bring a friend along to something that is on. Share a post so more people in the hills find us.
Join the member list

Or reply and tell us what you are into. One word is plenty: garden, shop, or workshop.

The Harvest is a community garden and creative gathering place taking shape in Witta, on Jinibara Country.

Whatever you turn up for, you are a member.
