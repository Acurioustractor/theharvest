## Center Of The Week

The place is starting to make sense because people are physically shaping it.

This week should feel practical, alive, and local. Not polished. Not grand. Show progress, ask for useful help, and invite people into the next layer.

## What People Should Understand

- The garden is moving from idea to visible progress.
- Small jobs are making the whole site easier to understand.
- The first version of The Harvest is being built from local effort, existing materials, and people showing up.
- The next useful step is not a big announcement. It is more hands, materials, photos, and local knowledge.

## This Week's Publishing Slate

### 1. The Garden Is Starting To Show Itself

Channel: Instagram reel plus Facebook post

Format: 20 to 30 second reel

Job: show visible progress and make the place feel real

Assets:

- before and after garden photos from 9 April
- pruning, weeding, mulch, road-facing cleanup
- any wide shot that shows the front garden or path

Caption:

```text
The garden is starting to show itself.

Not in a finished way. In the better way.

A few more weeds gone. A few more edges visible. A few more paths making sense. The kind of progress that only happens when people keep turning up and doing the useful jobs.

This is probably how The Harvest gets built.

One corner at a time.

If you live nearby and want to help shape the garden, send us a message.
```

CTA: `Message us if you want to help in the garden.`

GHL note: schedule to Instagram and Facebook. Use the reel as the primary asset.

### 2. Quick Local Ask

Channel: Facebook first, Instagram story second

Format: photo post or simple graphic

Job: convert attention into practical offers

Assets:

- empty garden corner
- tools, pots, mesh, mulch, chairs, or long table detail

Caption:

```text
Quick local ask.

We are collecting useful things for the first version of The Harvest.

Not fancy. Useful.

We are looking for:

- sturdy outdoor chairs
- folding tables
- timber that still has life in it
- garden tools
- large pots
- shade cloth
- mesh or trellis ideas for growing plants
- people who know gardens
- people who know kitchens

Before we buy new, we want to ask local.

If you have something sitting around that could help, send us a message.
```

CTA: `Send us a message with what you have.`

GHL note: publish on Facebook, then reuse as Instagram story tiles.

Notion record tags: `Harvest`, `Local Ask`, `Materials`, `Facebook`

## Permission And Safety

- Do not quote WhatsApp messages publicly without checking the person is happy with it.
- Treat WhatsApp names as internal unless approved.
- Use photos and videos only where people are comfortable being shown.
- Be careful with children or family moments. Use space and materials before faces.
- Do not overstate what The Harvest is yet. The honest line is that it is being shaped.
