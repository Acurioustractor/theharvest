- Any countdown graphic unless a new date is set.

Run these as small tests over two weeks. The goal is to learn what people respond to before scaling the calendar.

The building is still rough around the edges.

The Harvest is not arriving finished. It is being shaped in public, with the people who show up.

## First Newsletter Test

Subject:

```text
What is taking shape at The Harvest
```

Preview:

```text
A short note from the site, plus one thing we need.
```

Lead:

```text
The Harvest is in the useful middle.

Not an idea anymore. Not a finished place either.

There is a building to wake up, a garden to listen to, and a lot of small decisions that will shape what people feel when they first walk in.

That is the work right now. Keep it simple. Notice what is already here. Invite the right people in before the system gets too heavy.
```

Three updates:

```text
Garden
We are mapping what is already growing before deciding what to add.

Kitchen
The first food moments should stay simple while the bigger fit-out gets clearer.

Art Space
The best workshop ideas are the ones people already want to help make real.
```

Invitation:

```text
Reply with one thing you would love to see happen at The Harvest this winter.
```

## Test Review Questions

After two weeks, check:

- Which post got the most comments?
- Which post got the most useful offers?
- Which channel drove newsletter signups?
- Did people understand Garden, Kitchen, Art Space?
- Did any caption feel too polished or too vague?
- What questions kept coming back?

Turn the answers into the next two-week slate.
