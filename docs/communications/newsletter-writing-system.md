# Newsletter Writing System

## Job

The newsletter should make people feel close to the work and clear about the next useful action.

It is not a magazine. It is not a council update. It is a short note from the place.

## Standard Shape

1. Subject line
2. Preview text
3. Lead story
4. Three updates
5. One invitation
6. Footer

## GHL Build Order

1. Write the copy in Notion.
2. Choose one hero image.
3. Paste the copy into `newsletter-ghl-template.html`.
4. Replace the links.
5. Paste HTML into GoHighLevel.
6. Send a test to Ben and Nic.
7. Send to the right tag segment.
8. Mark the Notion row as `Published`.

## Audience Tags

Use Harvest-specific tags for sends:

- `harvest-newsletter`
- `harvest-local`
- `harvest-volunteer`
- `harvest-maker`
- `harvest-business`
- `harvest-event-attendee`

Do not send every Harvest newsletter to the generic `newsletter` tag unless the message is ACT-wide.

## Subject Lines

Good Harvest subject lines are plain and specific:

- What is growing this week
- The shed is nearly ready
- Saturday at The Harvest
- A callout for Witta makers
- Three things from the site this week

Avoid:
- Big announcement
- You will not believe this
- Our exciting journey continues
- Monthly newsletter

## Preview Text

Make it concrete:

- A working bee, a garden update, and one thing we need.
- The next gathering is taking shape.
- A short note from 9 Gumland Drive.

## Lead Story Formula

```text
[Plain opening sentence.]

[A real detail from the site, person, photo, or week.]

[What it means.]

[Invitation or next step.]
```

Example:

```text
The shed is starting to feel like a room again.

This week we cleared the back wall, opened up the side light, and started marking where the first long table could sit.

It is still rough. That is part of the point. The first version of The Harvest will be built from what is already here and what people bring with them.

If you have a folding table, old timber, or a few hours to help, reply to this email.
```

## Three Updates Formula

Each update should be no more than four lines:

```text
Garden
Barry has been walking us through what is already growing and what needs protection first.

Kitchen
We are keeping the first food moments simple while the longer fit-out takes shape.

Art Space
The first workshop ideas are coming from the people who have already put their hands up.
```

## CTA Rules

Use one main action per newsletter:

- RSVP
- reply with an idea
- volunteer
- share a photo
- submit a workshop
- read the compendium
- follow Instagram or Facebook

Do not stack five buttons. If everything is important, nothing is.
