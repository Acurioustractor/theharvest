# Staff feedback ask (post-opening)

Draft, not sent. For Trina, Dennis, and the stewards. Ben sends when ready. Voice checked: no em-dashes, no marketing words, "work days" not "working bees".

## Who and how

Small group, so keep it human. A short message (email or in person), not a form. Five questions, open answers, no scores. Send once, give a week, then talk it through together rather than only reading replies.

## The message

Subject: How is it going, honestly

Hi [name],

The Harvest is open and we are still finding our feet, which is exactly where we should be. Before the next stretch of work days and gatherings, I want to hear how it is landing for you, plainly. No right answers.

1. What worked well since we opened, that we should keep doing.
2. What did not work, or felt harder than it needed to be.
3. What is missing that would make your part of the place run better.
4. What are people telling you when they visit, good and bad.
5. One thing you would change before the next month.

Reply whenever suits, or grab me at the site. I will pull the answers together and we will talk through them as a group.

Thanks for building this with us.

Ben

## After replies come in

- Collect the answers into one short note, no names attached to specific gripes.
- Bring the themes to a group conversation, not a top-down summary.
- Anything operational becomes an action in the Harvest Actions DB (Notion), owned and dated.
- Anything about the place or programming feeds the next content and comms round.

## Note

This is a send action. It goes out only on Ben's go, from Ben, to the named people. Do not automate it or route it through GHL. It is a personal ask, not a campaign.
