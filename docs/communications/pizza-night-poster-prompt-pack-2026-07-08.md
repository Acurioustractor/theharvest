# Pizza night poster prompt pack

Created 2026-07-08.

Use this for ChatGPT Images, Canva AI, or any image/layout tool when making pizza night posters for The Harvest. Start from the real pizza photos in the repo. Do not generate fake Harvest scenes, fake crowds, fake interiors, fake QR codes, or fake text-heavy final art.

## Current source facts

- The Harvest is in Witta, on Jinibara Country.
- The poster is for DIY pizza at The Harvest.
- People can make their own pizza and cook it with the team.
- Standard pizza: $30 each.
- Toppings: cheeses, meats, and vegetables.
- Dessert pizza: Nutella, with whipped cream, strawberries, caramel sauce, or marshmallows.
- Dessert pizza: $10 each, or included with an order of 3 pizzas.
- Soft drinks: $5 to $6.50.

Use current dates or session times only when they are confirmed for the week.

## Recommended hierarchy

Do not put all the copy at the same size.

1. Biggest line: `DIY PIZZA AT THE HARVEST`
2. Second line: `Make it. Top it. Cook it with us.`
3. Price blocks:
   - `Pizza $30 each`
   - `Dessert pizza $10 each`
   - `Soft drinks $5 to $6.50`
4. Small line: toppings and dessert details.
5. Footer: `The Harvest, 9 Gumland Drive, Witta`

## Prompt 1: photo-led poster, portrait

Use this when uploading one of the real pizza photos to ChatGPT Images.

```text
Use the attached real photo as the dominant image. Create a warm, practical poster for The Harvest in Witta, on Jinibara Country.

Format: portrait poster, 1080 x 1350.

Design direction: real place first, hard edges, workmanlike type, warm milk paper background, dark shed brown, golden hour accent, small canopy green details. It should feel like a sign at the gate of a working community garden and pizza night, not a polished restaurant ad.

Keep the photo real. Crop and colour-correct only. Do not invent new people, new buildings, fake interiors, fake crowds, fake QR codes, cartoon pizza, Italian flag styling, or glossy cafe branding.

Use this exact copy:

DIY PIZZA AT THE HARVEST

Make it. Top it. Cook it with us.

Pizza $30 each

Dessert pizza $10 each
Nutella, whipped cream, strawberries, caramel sauce, or marshmallows.
Included with an order of 3 pizzas.

Soft drinks $5 to $6.50

The Harvest
9 Gumland Drive, Witta

Make the headline readable from a distance. Keep the details in clean blocks. Leave a clear bottom-right space for a real QR code to be added later. Do not generate a QR code.
```

## Prompt 4: background-only for final layout elsewhere

Use this if the image tool keeps damaging text. This is the safest route for print.

```text
Create a poster background only, using the attached real Harvest pizza photo as the base.

Format: portrait poster, 1080 x 1350.

Keep the photo real and recognisable. Add only simple Harvest-style graphic structure: milk paper margin, dark shed-brown text panels with no text inside them, golden hour divider lines, and a small canopy green accent. Leave large blank areas for headline, price blocks, details, and a real QR code to be added later.

No text. No logo. No QR code. No fake people. No invented venue scene. No cartoon pizza. No restaurant styling. No glossy effects.
```

## Final check before publishing

- All text is spelled correctly.
- No generated QR code is used.
- No fake people or fake Harvest scenes appear.
- Prices are still current.
- Session day and time are current for that week.
- If people are identifiable in the image, consent is clear.
- Final export has a source note for the real photo.
