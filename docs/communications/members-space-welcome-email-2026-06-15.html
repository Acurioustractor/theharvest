How to get in

It takes two minutes. Tap the button above, create a quick login with your name and email, and you will land on the Start Here page.

From there, do three things: fill in your profile, say hello in Garden Crew or The Shop Makers depending on what you are into, and open What's On to sign up for the weekend. There is a phone app too. Search Mighty Networks, then find The Harvest. The link above is the fastest way in.

What is on this weekend

Friday 19 June, 3pm to 5pm

Build the notice board. Our first gathering. We build the community notice board, make pizza, and shape how membership works together. Bring a bowl and cutlery to leave for Saturday. $10 to take part.

Saturday 20 June, from 10:30am

Founding members morning. A morning of fire and food. Pick the first harvest, build the tables, learn to make pizza, then share the first Harvest lunch together. Come along, free.

Saturday 20 June, from 5pm

The pizza night. The night we open The Harvest. A welcome, your Polaroid on the members wall, make-your-own pizza on the fire, and music into the evening. Live music from 3pm if you want to come early. $30 a head, kids free.

Booking and money

Joining the space is free. You only pay for the events you come to: Friday $10, and the Saturday pizza night $30, kids free. You book and pay inside the space, so there is nothing to sort on the day. More on how ongoing membership works soon.
