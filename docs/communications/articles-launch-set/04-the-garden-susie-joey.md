The garden at The Harvest is changing because two people keep showing up.

Susie and Joey are the paired Community Stewards taking shape on the site. They are on Harvest Pty's payroll, on Jinibara Country at Witta, and they are the reason the road-facing edge of the garden is starting to look like someone is caring for it again.

### Wednesdays at the garden

Wednesday is the day. It has been the day for a while. The Wednesday Maintenance Crew started before Susie and Joey were named, and it will keep going under and around them. What is different now is that there is a rhythm a visitor can feel from the gate.

A bed gets cleared. A path gets edged. Mulch moves from the pile to where it is needed. The kettle goes on. Someone notices that the trellis on the south side will not survive Windy Witta and starts thinking about what to do about it.

This is not yet a programmed garden. It is a garden being made legible to itself, one corner at a time.

### What stewardship looks like at this scale

Stewardship is a word that can land too grand. At The Harvest, on Wednesdays, it is more practical than that.

It looks like:

- The garden has the same two faces appearing each week, so it knows what continuity feels like
- The Wednesday crew has someone who knows where the watering points are and which beds are due
- Visiting locals who drop in with a load of compost or a cutting can be received by someone who knows the place
- The next person who joins the Wednesday rhythm can be welcomed in rather than left to figure it out

That is stewardship at the soft-opening scale. The garden does not need a master plan. It needs hands that come back.

### What the garden is becoming

The garden at The Harvest is not finished. It is not going to be finished for a long time. The point is not the finishing.

Right now the garden is taking shape:

- The road-facing beds are visibly tidier than they were in March
- The Wednesday crew is widening, slowly, with regulars and drop-ins
- The kids' corner of the garden is being held loosely while we figure out what kids actually want there
- The Milk Create Pavilion sits over the gathering, with the garden running out around it

Some of this work will be visible at the first soft opening on Saturday 20 June 2026. Member-list only for that one. The bigger public day comes later in the year, once the operating rhythm is real.

### How to be in the garden

The garden is not yet open to the public.

If you would like to be in it before then, the Wednesday crew is the way in. Send a note via the contact page if you are local and want to join a Wednesday. Bring a hat. Wear something you do not mind getting dirty. Stay an hour or stay until the kettle goes off.

If you are not local but want to follow what comes next, the member list is the way. We will write properly when there is something to write about.
