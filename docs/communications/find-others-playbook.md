# Find Others Playbook — Local channels for The Harvest

**Parent doc:** [docs/strategy/harvest-launch-alignment-2026-05-22.md](../strategy/harvest-launch-alignment-2026-05-22.md)
**Timing:** This playbook **activates after 20 June 2026**, post-proof-night. Before then, the soft opening stays member-list only (Path A). The exception is one-to-one invites from crew to friends-of-Harvest — those are warm asks, not playbook moves.

**Why staged this way:** the proof night needs to be the operating-system test, not the audience-acquisition test. Mixing the two means we learn nothing clean about either. Once the operating rhythm is real, the find-others phase opens the gate wider, knowing it can hold what shows up.

## Goal

Move The Harvest from "list of 101 contacts" to "known fixture in Witta and the hinterland." Within 12 months, the member list should be 300-500 people and the regular public openings should pull 80-150 each time without depending on Ben + Nic's individual networks.

Three audiences, in priority order:

1. **Witta locals** (the immediate ridge — postcode 4552, 4552 surrounds, and people who treat Maleny as their service town). Highest-value, hardest to reach via internet. They show up when their neighbours show up.
2. **Sunshine Coast hinterland** (Maleny, Mapleton, Montville, Conondale, Kenilworth, Eumundi, Yandina). Medium-value, easier to reach. Open to events but want to know what The Harvest *is* before they drive 20 minutes.
3. **Brisbane + sea-change visitors** (weekend audience, second-home owners). Lower priority for the community fabric, but they can fund the public events through paid offerings later.

Don't try to grow all three at once. Witta first. Hinterland second. Visitor audience last and only as a paid layer.

## Channels — Witta locals

### One: Witta Hall and the printed noticeboard

- **What:** Witta Community Hall has a physical noticeboard and a printed monthly newsletter that goes out via the residents' email list and physical drop.
- **Ask:** Get a one-page printed flyer on the noticeboard. Get a short note in the monthly newsletter. Both are free; both reach people who don't see Facebook.
- **Who to contact:** Witta Community Association (the hall's body). Find the current president via the hall's noticeboard; check Sonas + Luff network — they almost certainly know the right person.
- **Ben/Nic action:** One coffee with the WCA president. Bring a one-pager about The Harvest. Ask if a regular short update can go in the newsletter monthly.
- **Cadence:** Monthly update. ~150 words. Tone: news, not marketing.
- **Tier:** 1 (one-to-one ask, easily reversed). Owner: Nic (closer to Witta).

### Two: Witta Pub / The Witta General Store

- **What:** The two physical gathering points that aren't The Harvest. Both have customer foot traffic from the same audience.
- **Ask:** A small printed card on the counter pointing to /membership or to the soft opening (depending on phase). A relationship with whoever runs each, so when someone asks "what's happening at The Harvest now?" they have an honest answer.
- **Cadence:** Refresh the card monthly. Stop in for a chat every 2-3 weeks.
- **Tier:** 1. Owner: Joey or Susie (they're more often in the village than Ben).

### Three: The Wednesday Maintenance Crew network

- **What:** The existing Wednesday crew (Susie, Joey, and the rotating regulars) are the densest tie to the local community. Each crew member knows 5-15 people who'd come to an open day if invited personally.
- **Ask:** A printed seed-list — "if you'd like a friend or neighbour to know about The Harvest, here's a one-pager you can hand them." Each crew member takes 3-5. Personal, not viral.
- **Cadence:** Refresh the seed sheet quarterly.
- **Tier:** 1. Owner: Ben to design the seed sheet; Susie + Joey to distribute.

### Four: The school gate at Maleny State School and Witta-area kids' parents

- **What:** Parents of primary-age kids are the second-densest community network in the area, after the Wednesday crew. The Harvest's "kids' corner" is the natural hook for them.
- **Ask:** Once the kids' corner has actually hosted a couple of co-design afternoons (post-20-June), a short note to the school P&C asking if there's space for The Harvest in the school's monthly newsletter — framed as "a place for kids in the village", not "an event venue."
- **Cadence:** Quarterly mention.
- **Tier:** 1 (relationship ask). Owner: Whoever has the closest school-gate tie. Could be Susie or a crew member with kids at the school.

## Channels — Sunshine Coast hinterland

### Five: Local Facebook groups (specific, not generic)

The good ones for the hinterland audience, in priority order:

| Group | Why | Posting cadence | Permission to post |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maleny Community Page** (~10K members) | The hinterland's main bulletin board. People expect community-event posts. | Once per opening. No more. | Free, no permission needed for events. |
| **Maleny Hinterland Community Notice Board** (~5K) | Lower-volume than Maleny Community Page, higher engagement per post. | Once per opening. | Free. |
| **Sunshine Coast Hinterland Events** | Event-specific. People watch this group for what to do on a Saturday. | Once per opening, 2 weeks out. | Free. |
| **Witta Locals** (small, ~500) | Direct line to the audience that matters most. | Sparingly. Once a month max. | Free, but tone matters more here than reach. |
| **Maleny Buy/Sell/Swap/Give** | Not for events. For materials asks ("does anyone have a load of compost they can spare?") | When there's a specific ask. | Free, but stay on-topic. |

**What to post:** event-shaped post with one photo, plain language, no marketing copy. Always link to either the website or to a "reply to this if interested" comment thread.

**What NOT to post:** abstract "come to The Harvest" posts. People in Facebook groups respond to specific dates + a specific reason to show up.

**Tier:** 1 (one-time post is reversible; you can always delete it). Owner: Ben for setup, Susie or Joey for the ongoing local-voice posts.

### Six: Sunshine Coast Council community grants + events calendar

- **What:** SCC maintains an events calendar at sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/events and a community grants programme. The events calendar is a natural fit for any public opening. The grants programme is a funding-stream addition (see [working-capital-plan.md](../../thoughts/wiki/operations/working-capital-plan.md) if it exists).
- **Cadence:** Submit each public event 6 weeks ahead. Apply to community grants quarterly.
- **Tier:** 1. Owner: Ben.

### Seven: Maleny Co-op and farmers market network

- **What:** The Maleny Co-op (food + retail co-op in the town centre) has a strong local relationship with food producers. The Maleny Farmers Market runs weekly. Both are natural partners for The Harvest's shop and food story.
- **Ask:** A partner conversation, not a marketing ask. "We're building a thing at Witta with a shop layer. Can we visit, learn, possibly cross-promote when the shop is live?"
- **Cadence:** One conversation in June. Follow-up when the shop is real. Quarterly thereafter.
- **Tier:** 1 (relationship ask). Owner: Ben + Nic together — both bring different value to the conversation.

### Eight: Hinterland makers + craftspeople network

- **What:** Existing hinterland creative-economy networks. Sunshine Coast Arts, Mapleton Arts Council, the Maleny Wood Expo crowd, the Sunshine Coast Open Studios circuit. These are people who'd care about the Art Space residency programme.
- **Ask:** A listening conversation per person. No pitches yet. Build the relationship before the residency programme launches.
- **Cadence:** 2-3 conversations a month, sustained over 6 months.
- **Tier:** 1. Owner: Nic primarily (his network).

## Channels — Brisbane + visitor audience (last, only when ready)

### Nine: The newsletter forward mechanic

- **What:** Add a "forward to one neighbour you'd like to bring" line at the bottom of every member newsletter post-launch. Track new members tagged with `referred-by-member` (needs GHL workflow setup, see [launch-countdown-comms-cadence.md §automation still to wire](launch-countdown-comms-cadence.md)).
- **Why this is for visitors not locals:** locals don't forward emails. The forward mechanic mostly reaches the wider Brisbane diaspora that already knows somebody in the hinterland.
- **Cadence:** Standing CTA on every newsletter, starting July 2026.
- **Tier:** 1. Owner: Ben.

### Ten: Sea-change / hinterland weekend press

- **What:** The Sunshine Coast hinterland is well-covered by Brisbane lifestyle media (Broadsheet, Concrete Playground, Sunshine Coast Daily). They'll pick up a thoughtful piece about a hinterland community space if the angle is right. The angle is **not** "new venue opens." The angle is **"the story of the milk crate pavilion"** or **"what dairy heritage looks like as architecture."**
- **Cadence:** One thoughtful press placement per quarter, max. Quality over quantity.
- **Tier:** 2 (visible reach, hard to retract). Owner: Defer until the operating rhythm is established. Earliest pitch window: October 2026.

## What NOT to use

- Paid Facebook ads. The Harvest's growth needs to feel relational. Paid social inverts that.
- Generic email lists from third-party providers. Same reason.
- Influencer partnerships. Not the right voice.
- Eventbrite, Humanitix, etc. for ticketing during Year 1. Reply-led RSVP is the right cadence for Year 1. Add ticketing only when the paid event mechanics actually require it.

## Asks that travel well

When the crew is having one-to-one conversations, three asks travel well:

1. **"Are there 3 people you think would care about what we're building?"** — soft, no pressure. Builds the warm-introduction pipeline.
2. **"Do you have a Witta-area neighbour you'd like to bring to the next opening?"** — specific, easy yes/no.
3. **"What's the thing you wish existed in the hinterland that doesn't?"** — listening. Doesn't ask for anything, learns a lot.

These work as conversation seeds, not as scripts. Avoid asking "would you like to come to our event?" — it's a low-yield ask.

## How to track which channels are working

Two signals per channel:

| Channel | Signal 1 (volume) | Signal 2 (quality) |
|---|---|---|
| Witta Hall noticeboard | New member signups that name "noticeboard" in `source` | Replies/conversations from those signups |
| Witta Pub/General Store | New member signups in person via crew | Same |
| Maleny Community Page (FB) | New member signups in the 48hrs after a post | Comments + reactions on the post |
| Newsletter forward | New members tagged `referred-by-member` | Whether the forwarded member also forwards onward |
| One-to-one asks | Warm intros logged in Notion or a `manual-intros.md` doc | Whether the warm intro becomes a regular |

A channel that drives signups but no engagement is a low-quality channel. A channel that drives 5 signups who each become regulars is a high-quality channel. Optimize for quality.

**Practical tracking:** add a "How did you hear about The Harvest?" field to the member signup form. It's optional, and most people won't fill it in honestly. The signal is in the trend, not the individual answer.

Add the field with three preset options + an open text field:
- "A friend or neighbour told me"
- "Social media (Facebook or Instagram)"
- "In Witta — sign, poster, or saw the place"
- Open: "Something else"

Tier: 1 (field add). Owner: Ben.
