After three years of conversations, sketches, a lease, and a community of people who kept showing up, theharvestwitta.com.au is where The Harvest now lives.

What is on the site

Five things to look at first

The home page. Garden, Make, Feed. Three zones. The aerial video. A first read of who and what. See it.

People of The Harvest. The first storyteller profiles are up. Real people, in their own words. We are publishing more every week. Meet them.

The Collection. Five works. The Garden, the Milk Crate Pavilion, the Cedar, the Sauna, the Shop. Each one carries a thread back to the history of this place. Walk the works.

Gather. What is on, when, and how to come. The Garden Launch is the one to put in your diary first. See what is on.

Garden Launch + Community Day
Saturday 20 June 2026

10am to 3pm. 9 Gumland Drive, Witta QLD 4552. Jinibara Country. Sunshine Coast Hinterland. Free.

We will walk the new garden beds together. The Milk Crate Pavilion built by 80 of you in March opens to the public for the first time. There is a long table lunch, wood fired and hinterland grown. The kids plan their own corner with chalk and markers. The open mic asks one question: what is missing in Witta? We listen and write it down.

BYO seat or bring an empty one for a neighbour.

Quiet asks

Three things you can do this week

1. Share theharvestwitta.com.au with one person who would care about this. Local growers, makers, neighbours, family.

2. If you have stories about Witta, the dairy days, the timber years, the cooperative, send them. They go on the Witta page with your name and your words.

3. If you make something locally and want to be in the next round of the Shop, write back. We are listening.

Come by. Say hello. Bring something to share.
