# Launch Social Media Posts — The Harvest

5 posts for launch. Use across Facebook, Instagram, and local community groups (Maleny Community, Witta & District, Sunshine Coast Hinterland).

## Post 1: The Announcement
**Platform:** Facebook + Instagram + Local groups
**Image suggestion:** Aerial drone photo of the site
**Best time:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 9am

> **Witta has nowhere to gather. We're changing that.**
>
> On 5 acres of Jinibara Country, between the dairy farms and the rainforest, The Harvest is taking root.
>
> A community kitchen. Weekend markets. Workshop spaces. A native nursery that's been here for 27 years. And room for whatever this community wants to build.
>
> We're not a venue. We're not a brand. We're a gathering place — built by and for the people who show up.
>
> This is the beginning. Come see what's growing.
>
> theharvestwitta.com.au/compendium
>
> #TheHarvestWitta #Witta #SunshineCoastHinterland #Maleny #CommunityBuilding #EatGatherMakeGrow

## Post 2: Barry's Story (Community Spotlight)
**Platform:** Facebook + Instagram
**Image suggestion:** Photo of Barry at the nursery / the shed
**Best time:** Saturday, 10am

> **Barry has been restoring this nursery for 27 years. He doesn't own it. He just couldn't let it go.**
>
> When we first walked onto the site in Witta, the nursery was the thing that stopped us. Rows of native species — some rare, some local, all planted by one person over nearly three decades.
>
> Barry is the kind of person every community has but rarely celebrates. He just showed up and kept going.
>
> The Harvest is being built around people like Barry. Not around a business plan.
>
> More of Barry's story: theharvestwitta.com.au/stories
>
> #TheHarvestWitta #CommunityStories #Witta #NativeNursery #HinterlandPeople

## Post 4: Residency Program
**Platform:** Instagram + Facebook
**Image suggestion:** The site at golden hour / workshop space concept
**Best time:** Wednesday, 12pm

> **Come create something.**
>
> The Harvest is launching an artist and enterprise in residence program in Witta, Sunshine Coast Hinterland.
>
> If you're an artist, maker, writer, social entrepreneur, or someone with a project that needs space and community around it — this might be your place.
>
> Come for a few weeks. Work on your thing. Share meals with the community. Leave something behind that matters.
>
> Inspired by the Bauhaus movement — where artists and builders lived and worked side by side.
>
> Applications open: theharvestwitta.com.au/get-involved
>
> #ArtistResidency #EnterpriseInResidence #TheHarvestWitta #CreativeResidency #Bauhaus #MakerSpace #SunshineCoast
