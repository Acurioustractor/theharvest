Witta has nowhere to gather.
We're changing that.

On 5 acres of Jinibara Country, between the dairy farms and the rainforest, something is taking root. Not a development. Not a brand. A gathering place.

The Harvest is a place to eat, gather, make, and grow. A community kitchen where locals cook together. A nursery where Barry has been restoring native species for 27 years. Workshop spaces for people who make things with their hands. Market grounds where Hinterland producers meet the people who eat what they grow.

We're building for people who want to belong without having to perform.
