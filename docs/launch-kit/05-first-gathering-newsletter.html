The story

When colonizers arrived in Moreton Bay, they blew up the Aboriginal oyster leases for limestone — to build the Treasury, the banks, the city itself.

Shaun Fisher is bringing oysters back. Farmed on Quandamooka Country, sold direct to community. The shells will be collected and used by our architect to make rammed earth flooring. Full circle.

Regional Arts Australia

We've been funded to explore the dairy, timber and co-op heritage of the Blackall Range — through photography, oral histories, and building things from recycled materials.

Got old photos? Equipment? Stories about Witta's past? We want to hear from you. Reply to this email or come tell us on the day.
