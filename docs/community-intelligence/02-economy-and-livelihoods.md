## Enrichment Prompts

> [!question] What industries employ the 789 Professionals?
> Census tells us occupation category but not employer. Are they self-employed consultants working from home? Commuting to Sunshine Coast hospitals? Running hinterland businesses? Field research needed.

> [!question] How many of the 130 food/hospitality-trained residents are currently working in that field?
> Field of study does not equal current employment. Some may have pivoted. Some may want to return. The Kitchen zone could be the path back.

> [!question] What does the $370/week median rent look like in 2025?
> This is 2021 data. Post-COVID rental increases in the Hinterland have been severe. Current median rent is likely significantly higher. Cross-reference with realestate.com.au or SQM Research for current figures.

> [!question] Where are the 84 Machinery Operators and Drivers working?
> Forestry? Transport? Agriculture? Mining commuters? This is a small but distinct cohort — understanding their needs helps design relevant community services.

> [!question] What proportion of the 457 Managers are farm/property managers vs corporate?
> The Manager category in Census includes both farm managers and corporate executives. In a rural postcode like 4552, the mix matters for understanding the community's economic character.
