# Biodiversity & Threatened Species

**Geographic context**: This chapter draws on the Atlas of Living Australia (ALA) occurrence records within a 10 km radius of Witta (-26.75, 152.85), covering the Sunshine Coast Hinterland from the Blackall Range through surrounding valleys and creek systems. Total records: **231,593** across 22 species groups.

See also: [[00-index]] | [[03-community-organizations]] | [[11-harvest-opportunities]]

99 threatened species have been recorded within the 10 km radius. Below are the top 50 by record count, grouped by conservation status.

> [!insight] Globally significant extinction records
> Two Extinct in the Wild species -- the Gastric-brooding Frog and Southern Day Frog -- were last recorded in this area. Both are internationally significant losses. Their historical presence here adds weight to conservation narratives and demonstrates the ecological importance of the Blackall Range.

## Strategic Relevance

> [!question] Partner with Barung Landcare for citizen science?
> Barung Landcare (see [[03-community-organizations]]) already runs revegetation and monitoring programs across the Blackall Range. A formal partnership could support biodiversity monitoring on The Harvest site, strengthen grant applications with established org credibility, and provide volunteer networks for planting days.

> [!question] Biodiversity walks as Garden zone programming?
> With 151K bird records and 99 threatened species in the radius, guided biodiversity walks could be a signature Garden zone experience. Low cost to run, high community engagement, and directly demonstrates the site's ecological values to visitors and potential funders.
