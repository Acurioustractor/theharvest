## Sentiment Analysis

### Dominant Positive Themes Across All Venues

Themes extracted from review text across the full venue set, ranked by frequency of appearance in "top themes" columns:

| Theme | Venues Where Top Theme | Notes |
|-------|----------------------:|-------|
| **food_quality** | 14 | By far the most common positive signal -- appears across cafes, markets, producers, and even community venues |
| **local_identity** | 12 | Reviewers value the sense of place, provenance, and "local feel" |
| **community_value** | 8 | Gathering, belonging, and civic contribution themes |
| **nature_environment** | 5 | Concentrated in nature reserves and gardens, but also Maleny Showground |
| **family_friendly** | 2 | Maleny Dairies and Maleny Hotel -- venues that welcome kids |
| **arts_culture** | 4 | Crystal Waters, Rosetta Books, Barung Landcare, Montville Markets |

> [!insight]
> **Food is the #1 positive theme across venues -- validates Kitchen zone.** Of 20 surveyed venues, 14 have food_quality as a top review theme. This is not limited to restaurants and producers; even community venues like Maleny Neighbourhood Centre and Maleny RSL draw food-related praise. The Harvest's Kitchen zone is directly aligned with what visitors and locals already value most.

> [!insight]
> **Community value consistently appears in top themes -- people want gathering spaces.** Eight venues surface community_value as a dominant positive theme, from the Co-op to the Showground to the Neighbourhood Centre. Reviewers talk about belonging, welcome, and civic contribution. There is demonstrated demand for spaces that serve a gathering function beyond pure commerce.

> [!insight]
> **Witta Markets (4.6 stars, 158 reviews) = The Harvest's closest benchmark.** Same locality, same ethos of local food and community gathering. The Harvest can study what Witta Markets does well (country atmosphere, shade, grassy oval) and extend it into a permanent, multi-zone offering with deeper programming.

## Observations for The Harvest

- **Food-first positioning works.** The review data shows food is the single strongest attractor across every venue category. Leading with Kitchen zone programming (market days, communal meals, producer showcases) gives The Harvest the highest-probability entry point.
- **Community venues punch above their weight on sentiment.** Maleny Neighbourhood Centre (4.9 stars) and Maple Street Co-op (4.7 stars) have fewer reviews but higher ratings than the big tourism draws. Small-scale, values-led venues earn outsized loyalty.
- **Nature and place matter.** Five venues surface nature_environment as a theme. The Harvest's rural Witta setting is an asset, not a limitation -- reviewers actively seek the hinterland atmosphere.
- **Arts and culture is underserved.** Only four venues surface arts_culture, and three of those (Crystal Waters, Barung Landcare, Montville Markets) are not primarily arts venues. There is white space for a dedicated creative programming offer -- the Art Space zone.
