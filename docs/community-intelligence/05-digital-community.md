# Digital Community Landscape

**Context**: This chapter maps the active Facebook groups and pages that form the digital public square for the Maleny--Witta--Hinterland community. These spaces represent where local conversations happen, where needs surface, and where The Harvest can listen, learn, and eventually participate. Data is based on manual survey of public Facebook groups and pages as of early 2026.

**Total community reach: ~72,000 members/followers** (with significant overlap between groups).

> [!insight] 72K total reach = significant digital community presence
> Even accounting for overlap (many locals belong to multiple groups), this represents a substantial digital footprint for a hinterland population of ~7,300. The ratio suggests strong digital engagement and a community that actively uses Facebook as its primary coordination platform. Any digital engagement strategy for The Harvest must account for these existing spaces rather than trying to replace them.

> [!insight] Witta Community (500 members) = hyper-local audience
> The Witta Community group is small but directly relevant -- these are The Harvest's immediate neighbours. Early engagement here (listening, not promoting) builds the social licence that larger-reach groups cannot provide. This is where trust is built or lost.

> [!insight] Range Gardeners aligns perfectly with Garden zone
> At ~3,000 members focused on food growing, permaculture, native plants, and composting, Range Gardeners is a natural ally for The Harvest's Garden zone. Knowledge sharing, seed swaps, and seasonal growing workshops could bridge online community into on-site participation.

> [!question] Enrichment prompts
> What percentage of these groups' members are located within the Maleny--Witta postcode (4552) vs. broader Sunshine Coast? Are there Instagram or other platform communities not captured here? What is the posting frequency and engagement rate (likes/comments per post) in the top groups?

> None of The Harvest's three zones lack an existing online audience. The challenge is not demand generation -- it is curation and invitation. The digital community is already talking about what The Harvest wants to build. The opportunity is to become a physical home for conversations that are currently only happening online.

> [!question] Strategic prompts
> Which groups should The Harvest engage with first? What is the right sequence -- listen, participate, invite? Are there group admins who could become early allies or advisors? Could The Harvest host a "community listening session" seeded from themes surfaced in these groups?
