# Events & Markets

The Sunshine Coast Hinterland has a strong calendar of recurring markets, annual festivals, and tourism events. Understanding this landscape is essential for positioning The Harvest -- both to participate in what exists and to identify programming gaps worth filling.

## Event Details

### 1. Witta Markets

- **Type**: Community market
- **Schedule**: First Saturday of every month, 6:30am--12pm
- **Location**: Witta Recreation Club, Witta
- **Description**: Local produce, plants, crafts, food stalls, and live music. A relaxed, family-friendly gathering that draws the immediate Witta and surrounding community. The closest recurring event to The Harvest's site.

> [!insight] Closest community touchpoint
> Witta Markets is The Harvest's nearest regular community event -- both geographically and culturally. This is the natural first place to build presence, share what we're doing, and recruit early supporters.

---

### 2. Maleny Community Markets

- **Type**: Community market
- **Schedule**: Every Sunday, 8am--1pm
- **Location**: RSL Hall, Maleny
- **Description**: Weekly market featuring local produce, handmade goods, and a strong community atmosphere. The most frequent market in the region and a reliable weekly gathering point.

---

### 3. Montville Markets

- **Type**: Artisan and produce market
- **Schedule**: Second Saturday of every month
- **Location**: Montville Village Green
- **Description**: Artisan and produce market in the tourist-facing village of Montville. Draws a mix of locals and visitors, with an emphasis on handmade and boutique goods.

---

### 4. Maleny Show

- **Type**: Annual agricultural event
- **Schedule**: Annually, typically May or June
- **Location**: Maleny Showground
- **Description**: Annual agricultural show celebrating rural life, livestock, local produce, and community spirit. A long-standing tradition that brings together the broader hinterland community.

---

### 5. Maleny Music Festival

- **Type**: Multi-day music and arts festival
- **Schedule**: Annual, New Year period
- **Location**: Various venues across Maleny
- **Description**: Multi-day festival spanning music, arts, workshops, and community gatherings. One of the hinterland's highest-profile cultural events, drawing visitors from across the region and beyond.

---

### 6. Maleny Wood Expo

- **Type**: Annual exhibition
- **Schedule**: Annually (dates vary)
- **Location**: Maleny Showground
- **Description**: Exhibition of woodworking, timber arts, and sustainable forestry. Celebrates the region's strong tradition of craft and connection to the land.

---

### 7. Sunshine Coast Hinterland Harvest Trail

- **Type**: Tourism / farm gate trail
- **Schedule**: Ongoing and seasonal
- **Location**: Various farms across the hinterland
- **Description**: A curated trail connecting visitors with local producers -- farm gates, tastings, and behind-the-scenes experiences. Positions the hinterland as a food and agritourism destination.

---

### 8. Maleny Scarecrow Festival

- **Type**: Community event
- **Schedule**: Annually, typically June
- **Location**: Maple Street and surrounds, Maleny
- **Description**: Community festival with scarecrow displays, workshops, and street activities. A playful, family-oriented event that brings life to Maleny's main street.

---

## Insights

> [!insight] 3 regular markets = strong market culture
> The hinterland sustains three recurring markets (Witta monthly, Maleny weekly, Montville monthly). This signals a community that shows up regularly to buy local, connect, and participate -- a strong foundation for anything The Harvest wants to do around food, produce, and gathering.

> [!insight] $54M annual economic impact
> Eumundi Markets alone generates an estimated $54M in annual economic impact (per local media). The hinterland's market and event culture is not just community glue -- it is a significant economic engine.
