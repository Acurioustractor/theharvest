## Key Insights

> [!insight] The Strongest Case
> Kitchen zone has the strongest public sentiment data — food is the #1 positive theme across ALL venue reviews. Start here for quick wins and community buy-in.

> [!insight] The Grant Opportunity
> Arts and Heritage Levy is the most actionable funding pathway. 157 creative workers + 24 arts media articles + Arts Council = ready-made Art Space case.

> [!insight] The Environmental Story
> 231K biodiversity records + 99 threatened species (including 2 Extinct in Wild) makes the Garden zone a conservation narrative powerhouse. Partner with Barung Landcare.

> [!insight] The Community Voice
> Six data-backed personas now bring the numbers to life — from Margaret the garden steward to Kai the 28-year-old priced out of his hometown. See [[13-personas-and-needs]] for the full engagement strategy, and [[14-human-insights-synthesis]] for the raw human insights across all datasets. A live pulse survey at `/pulse` captures ongoing community voice.

> [!question] Geographic Note
> Population (ERP) data uses SA2 "Maroochy Hinterland" (316061443). Census data uses POA 4552 (Maleny/Witta postcode, larger area). These don't perfectly overlap — the SA2 is a subset of the postcode area.
