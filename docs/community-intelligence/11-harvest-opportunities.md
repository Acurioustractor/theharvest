## Grant Evidence Pack

This section compiles the strongest data points for grant applications.

### Community Need

- Population growing 33% over 24 years (5,489 → 7,316) with no new community hub — [[01-demographics]]
- Median age 56 = ageing population needing gathering spaces — [[01-demographics]]
- 34.7% aged 65+ = significant retiree population — [[01-demographics]]
- Housing affordability crisis (90/95 suburbs >$1M median) driving social isolation — [[06-media-landscape]]
- Council strategies explicitly call for more community gathering spaces — [[10-council-and-policy]]

### Community Assets

- 19 community organizations ready for partnership — [[03-community-organizations]]
- 72,000 total digital community reach — [[05-digital-community]]
- 3 regular markets demonstrating community gathering culture — [[04-events-and-markets]]
- 30+ venues with strong positive sentiment — [[08-venues-and-reviews]]

### Environmental Significance

- 231,593 biodiversity records in 10km radius — [[07-biodiversity]]
- 99 threatened species including 5 Critically Endangered — [[07-biodiversity]]
- 2 Extinct in Wild species (globally significant) — [[07-biodiversity]]
- Koala population (Endangered, 566+ records) — [[07-biodiversity]]
- Strong environmental org network (Barung Landcare, Green Hills Fund, Bush Links) — [[03-community-organizations]]

### Economic Impact

- 2,589 employed persons in POA 4552 — [[02-economy-and-livelihoods]]
- $54M annual market economy (Eumundi benchmark) — [[06-media-landscape]]
- Hinterland Harvest Trail tourism infrastructure — [[04-events-and-markets]]
- Cooperative economy heritage (Credit Union, Maple Street Co-op) — [[03-community-organizations]]

### Policy Alignment

- Sunshine Coast Community Strategy 2019-2041 — [[10-council-and-policy]]
- Arts and Heritage Levy (direct funding pathway) — [[10-council-and-policy]]
- Environment and Liveability Strategy — [[10-council-and-policy]]
- Reconciliation Action Plan — [[10-council-and-policy]]
- Neighbourhood and Targeted Community Projects (OPEN consultation) — [[10-council-and-policy]]

> [!question] What's missing from this evidence pack?
> - Direct community survey data from Witta residents
> - Letters of support from partner organisations
> - Economic modelling for The Harvest's projected impact
> - Comparative analysis with similar community hubs (Crystal Waters, Eumundi)
