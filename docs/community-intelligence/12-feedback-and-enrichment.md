# Feedback & Enrichment — Growing the Knowledge Base

> Back to [[00-index]]

This knowledge base is a living document. The 14 research datasets give us a foundation, but the most valuable intelligence comes from **direct community engagement**. This chapter outlines how to gather more data, give back to the community, and keep this resource current.

---

## Community Data Sharing

### Witta Markets Booth

The Witta Markets (first Saturday, 6:30am–12pm) are The Harvest's closest community touchpoint — [[04-events-and-markets]].

**Setup**: A simple table with printed highlights from this knowledge base, plus a feedback box.

**What to share**:
- "Did you know?" cards: 231K species records, 99 threatened species, 24 years of population growth
- Community map showing organisations, venues, and events from [[03-community-organizations]] and [[04-events-and-markets]]
- Printed biodiversity highlights with local species photos — [[07-biodiversity]]

**What to gather**:
- "What does Witta need?" — open-ended feedback cards
- "What organisations are missing from our list?" — enrichment for [[03-community-organizations]]
- "What's your connection to this place?" — stories for [[09-heritage-and-history]]
- Contact details for people who want to stay involved

## Data Gathering

### Community Survey

The biggest gap in this knowledge base is **direct community voice**. The data tells us what exists; surveys tell us what people want.

**Suggested survey questions**:

1. How long have you lived in the Witta/Maleny area?
2. What do you value most about this community?
3. What's missing from the community — what do you wish existed?
4. Which of these would you use? (Garden space / Community kitchen / Art/maker space / Workshop space / Meeting rooms / Children's area)
5. How often would you visit a community hub in Witta?
6. What skills or knowledge would you share with others?
7. What barriers prevent you from participating in community activities?
8. Do you identify as Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander? (optional)
9. What age group are you in?

**Distribution**:
- Paper copies at Witta Markets, Maleny Neighbourhood Centre, Maple Street Co-op
- Digital version via Facebook groups (Witta Community, Maleny Community Board)
- QR code on printed materials linking to online form
- GHL form integration for data capture — see [[../launch-kit/]]

### Field Observations

Use the [[templates/field-observation]] template for structured on-the-ground research.

**Priority observation sites**:
- Witta Markets (first Saturday mornings)
- Witta Recreation Club (weekdays — is it used? By whom?)
- The Harvest site itself (what happens there naturally?)
- Maleny Maple Street (Saturday mornings — who's there, what do they do?)
- Gardners Falls (weekend afternoons — tourism vs local use)

**What to observe**:
- People count by time of day
- Demographics (families, retirees, young people, tourists)
- Activities (shopping, socialising, exercising, working)
- What's working well about the space
- What's missing or broken

## Giving Back to the Community

This knowledge base was built from public data about the community. The community should benefit from it.

### Biodiversity Walks

Partner with Barung Landcare ([[03-community-organizations]]) to offer guided biodiversity walks using data from [[07-biodiversity]].

- **What**: Monthly walks identifying species from the 231K ALA records
- **Where**: The Harvest site, Mary Cairncross, Gardners Falls area
- **Who leads**: Barung Landcare volunteers + local naturalists
- **Output**: Citizen science contributions back to ALA, community connection

### Heritage Storytelling

Partner with community elders to document oral histories before they're lost — [[09-heritage-and-history]].

- **What**: Recorded conversations with long-term residents about Witta/Maleny history
- **Where**: Maleny Neighbourhood Centre, Witta Recreation Club, homes
- **Who**: Maleny RSL Sub-Branch members, farming families, First Nations elders (with permission and cultural protocols)
- **Output**: Audio archive, transcripts, enrichment for [[09-heritage-and-history]]
- **Connection**: Trove gap — oral histories fill what written records don't capture

### Annual Community Report

Compile insights from this knowledge base into a public annual report.

- **What**: "State of Our Community" — key stats, trends, stories, opportunities
- **Format**: A4 printed booklet + digital version
- **Distribution**: Witta Markets, Maleny Neighbourhood Centre, Council, partner orgs
- **Timing**: Q4 each year, timed with community survey results
- **Purpose**: Demonstrate The Harvest's value as a community intelligence resource

### Open Data

Make this knowledge base available to other community organisations and researchers.

- **Obsidian vault** available to partners (Barung Landcare, Maleny Neighbourhood Centre, Council)
- **Research data** (anonymised) available on request
- **Methodology** documented so others can replicate for their communities

## Enrichment Workflow

When new data comes in, use these templates:

1. **New data point** → [[templates/enrichment-note]]
2. **Field visit** → [[templates/field-observation]]
3. **Update existing chapter** → Edit the relevant file directly, add date stamp
4. **New data source** → Add to quarterly refresh calendar above

### Quality Standards

- Every data point should cite its source (dataset, date, methodology)
- Distinguish between **verified data** (from official sources) and **community knowledge** (from conversations, observations)
- Flag any numbers that could be misinterpreted (e.g., POA 4552 ≠ Witta alone)
- Update the status tag when data becomes stale (>12 months old)
- Cross-reference new data with [[11-harvest-opportunities]] to identify new opportunities
