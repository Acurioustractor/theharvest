The Harvest's own structure and values sit squarely within this lineage -- worth referencing in grant applications and partnership conversations.

## Strategic Questions

> [!question] Which organizations would be natural partners for The Harvest?
> Consider alignment by zone: Garden (environment, landcare), Kitchen (co-ops, community service), Art Space (arts council, singers). Which 3-5 orgs would you approach first, and with what offer?

> [!question] Are there organizations missing from this list?
> This is a snapshot, not an exhaustive directory. Are there informal groups, Facebook communities, school P&Cs, church groups, or First Nations organizations that should be mapped? What about newer groups that have formed in the last 2-3 years?

> [!question] What does the type distribution tell us?
> Community Service and Environment are the largest categories (3 each). There is no dedicated food/agriculture organization listed -- is that a gap The Harvest could fill, or are there groups we have not yet mapped?
