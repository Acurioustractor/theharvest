# The Harvest — 20 June open day copy

Single source for the open-day copy: the website page, the short and micro versions, and the social teaser series. Hand this to whoever posts.

**Voice:** The Harvest voice (Curtis method: name the room, name the body, stop the line). No em dashes. No emoji as formatting. No AI tells (delve, vibrant, showcase, "not just X but Y").

**Event:** Saturday 20 June 2026, from 1pm (pizza from 5pm). 9 Gumland Drive, Witta QLD 4552, Jinibara Country. Free, everyone welcome.
**Landing page:** https://www.theharvestwitta.com.au/june-20 (lives in `client/src/pages/GardenLaunch.tsx`)

## Facts to stay anchored to (do not drift)

- This is a **first opening** of an old nursery, not a reopening. The gate has been shut a long time.
- The **gardens are established** ("growing things on this hill for a hundred years"). Not new beds.
- Witta **hasn't had a shop in a generation**. The shop is being put back, it never existed in living memory. Not a "reopening" of a shop.
- The site: **5 acres, an old nursery, a rammed earth building, a pavilion under the pecans**, gardens, paddocks.
- Witta: ~1,300 people, 10 minutes from Maleny, 2,000 cars through each weekend, nowhere to stop.
- The Harvest is a place to **eat, gather, make, grow**.
- The shop is a **shared shelf for local growers and makers**. Not a supermarket, not a boutique.
- Do **not** assert the pavilion build numbers ("80 helped build in March") until confirmed. It is the **Milk Crate Pavilion**, not "Milk Create".

**Hero**
Saturday 20 June 2026 · Witta · Jinibara Country
The Harvest Witta
The gate opens.
An old nursery on the hill becoming a place to eat, gather, make and grow. Come for the afternoon: walk the gardens, tell us what you'd put on the shop shelf, leave an idea, stay for pizza from five.
Open day for anyone who might grow, make or gather here.
[ I'm coming ] [ Get directions ]

**Facts:** Saturday 20 June 2026 · From 1pm, pizza from 5pm · 9 Gumland Drive, Witta · 10 min from Maleny · Everyone welcome
We acknowledge the Jinibara people as the Traditional Custodians of this Country. The day begins with a Welcome, and The Harvest is being shaped with that in mind.

**What it is — An old nursery, opening its gate.**
Witta has about 1,300 people and nowhere to stop. No shop in a generation, nowhere to gather. The Harvest is five acres on the hill: an old nursery with a rammed earth building and gardens that have grown things here for a hundred years.
On 20 June we open the gate for the afternoon. Come see the place. Walk the gardens. Tell us one thing: what you'd grow or make for the shop, how you'd use this space.
We write it all down, then the oven goes on under the pavilion.
Come for an hour or stay till dark. Bring a chair if you've got one.

**The shape of the day — Afternoon into fire.**
Free. No register, no bar. Kids welcome, there's a corner with paper and chalk.
- *From 1pm — Walk the place.* The gardens, the rammed earth building, the pavilion under the pecans. What's here, what's coming.
- *Through the afternoon — Two questions.* What you could grow or make for the shop shelf. How you'd use this space: market mornings, maker days, the kids' corner, the empty rooms. Leave it on the wall or tell us at the long table.
- *From 5pm — Pizza.* Three doughs, whatever the garden gave. Music. Stay till dark.

**Before the day — Three small ways in.**
- *I'm coming.* One tap so we make enough dough. [ I'm coming ]
- *I'd grow or make for the shop.* What's in your patch or on your bench. [ Put something on the shelf ]
- *I've got an idea.* The space, the kids' corner, a maker day. [ Share an idea ]
Free. We count the pizza by hand.

**What this is for — A shop that sells what your neighbour grew.**
Witta hasn't had a shop in a generation. The Harvest is putting one back: a shared shelf for the growers and makers who already live around Witta and Maleny. Not a supermarket, not a boutique. A shelf, kept simple on purpose.
It only works if it's full of local hands. You grow it or make it, we hold the shelf.
20 June is where we find out who's in. Come see if you are.
