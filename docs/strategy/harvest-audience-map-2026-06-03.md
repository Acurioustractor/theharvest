# The Harvest: four lanes of people — engage, path, content

> The simple operating map. Who comes, which lane they are on, what they carry in GHL, what to
> share with them, and the one thing never to do. Grounded in the orbit model
> (`act-global-infrastructure/wiki/concepts/energy-orbit.md` + `relationship-first-crm.md`): sort
> by energy not money, two lanes never one funnel, belonging before money. The day is the engine;
> this just catches and sorts the energy it makes.

## One line each
- **Public** comes to belong. Tag the gathering, share the open day, leave them be. Most stay, that is the win.
- **Makers / users** come to build. Give them a crew and a work day; they climb the rings by doing, hand-read.
- **Producers** come to sell on the shelf. A commercial pipe, gift-shaped pay, one to one.
- **Community** (Jinibara, elders, storytellers) we owe back. Never ring, score, or drip. Consent first.

A person can be in more than one lane at once. A potter who comes for pizza (public), helps build
the art space (maker), and puts mugs on the shelf (producer) is one human on three axes. That is
the model working, not a tagging mess.

## 1. General public — come to eat, gather, belong
- **Who:** Witta and Maleny neighbours, families, anyone who turns up.
- **Engage:** the `/june-20` page, the 6 public posts, word of mouth, the day itself.
- **Path:** relationship core, **outer ring (curious). Most stay here, and that is success.**
- **Carries:** `witta-gathering-2026-06-20`; `harvest-newsletter` only if they choose to follow.
- **Share with them:** the open day, the photo wall and recap after, then a seasonal story with no
  ask (the Receive rung). Welcome grammar, never a pitch.
- **Their get:** a good afternoon, a place to stop, belonging.
- **NEVER:** auto-climb them into a pipeline, drip them, or read a tap as a lead.

## 2. Makers / Harvest-users — come to use and build the place
- **Who:** people who want the garden, art space, events space, work days, kids' corner, residencies.
- **Engage:** the maker session, work days, the "I've got an idea" path (`/get-involved?form=idea`),
  the co-design and art-space posts.
- **Path:** relationship core, **climbing curious → connected → member → active → steward by DOING,
  hand-read.** The Harvest Membership Journey pipeline. Give them a **crew/pod** (garden crew,
  art-space crew), which is the bonding unit, not a pipeline stage.
- **Carries:** `rsvp-maker-morning`, interest tags (`interest-garden` / `interest-events` /
  `interest-volunteer` / `interest-workshops`), `idea:witta-2026-06`, a `pod:` tag for their crew;
  `harvest-member` only when they opt into mutual commitment (not money).
- **Share with them:** the Makers' invite, work-day invites, art-space and co-design posts,
  GetInvolved. "Help shape it, this becomes yours."
- **Their get:** a hand in building the place, a crew, their story shown and owned.
- **NEVER:** enroll them from an RSVP click; charge for a rung; drip without a real relationship.

## 3. Shop producers — come to put something on the shelf
- **Who:** growers and makers around Witta and Maleny who would sell on consignment.
- **Engage:** the shop EOI (`/shop`), the one-to-one maker outreach (Email A/B/DM in
  `research/data/harvest-shop-outreach-messages.md`), the "Book a chat about the shop" calendar, the day.
- **Path:** **The Shop pipeline. Commercial, money plumbing, a separate axis from the rings.**
  New interest → In conversation → Sampling / trial shelf → On the shelf.
- **Carries:** `harvest-shop-interest`, `shop-call-booked`, one offer tag (`shop-produce` /
  `shop-maker` / `shop-food` / `shop-consignment` / `shop-follow-up`), `shop-prospect` if imported.
- **Share with them:** the maker outreach copy (one to one, small batches, never a blast), the shop
  EOI page, the shop nurture. The gift-shaped offer: **you keep 75 to 80 percent, and we name you
  and say what you were paid.**
- **Their get:** a second outlet, fair pay, named on the shelf.
- **NEVER:** blast them; fuse the commercial pipe with the rings; drip a `lane:community` producer.

## 4. Community — Jinibara, elders, storytellers
- **Who:** the Jinibara custodians whose Country this is, elders, anyone whose name, story, or photo appears.
- **Engage:** the Welcome to Country, relationship, the day. Not a form.
- **Path:** **the constellation. `lane:community`. Measured by what we owe back, never by energy to ACT.**
- **Carries:** `lane:community` (plus `role:elder` / `is_storyteller` where true). A gathering tag
  for the headcount is fine; nothing else.
- **Share with them:** nothing as a funnel. Content flows the other way, we owe them. Any publish of
  a name, story, or photo runs **consent-check first** (OCAP gate).
- **Their get:** consent-held sovereignty, what we owe back.
- **NEVER:** ring them, energy-score them, drip them, or publish without consent.

## How the day feeds each lane (the catch)
The 20 June day is the energy engine. On the day and the morning after, a human (Ben + Nic) reads
who brought energy and sorts them by hand:
- tapped "I'm coming", ate, left happy → **public**, leave them be.
- helped, lingered, offered to come back → **maker/user**, invite to a work day or a crew, move
  toward Connected by hand.
- "I'd put eggs on the shelf" → **producer**, into The Shop pipeline at New interest.
- a Jinibara elder, a storyteller → **community**, `lane:community`, consent before anything.

The system catches the energy. The human reads it. No workflow climbs anyone automatically. That
is the whole discipline: belonging before money, two lanes never one funnel.
