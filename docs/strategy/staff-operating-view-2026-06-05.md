## The four screens (plus paper)

Everything a steward touches, ever:

| Screen | Job | The moves |
|---|---|---|
| **GHL app** (LeadConnector) | Relationships | Reply to messages, drag shop cards, book chats |
| **Square POS** | The till | Sell, track stock per maker, run payouts |
| **Humanitix organiser** | Events | Duplicate event, change date, tap names at the gate |
| **WhatsApp** | The crews | Garden / makers / build-day chatter |
| **Paper** | The room | Come-back sheet, kraft drop-off labels, the roster on the wall |

That is the entire system. Nothing else gets added unless one of these five demonstrably
breaks. (This table is the answer to the platform question from the staff seat: a community
platform would be a sixth surface that does none of these five jobs.)

## Job 1 — The shop: engage the seller, organise the assets, set the times

**Engage (exists, runs in GHL):** EOI or the Knock → Shop pipeline card → "book a chat"
calendar → real conversation → drag the card. Monday sweep catches anyone going quiet.
Shop nurture (spec 6) sends the don't-go-cold second touch automatically.

**Onboard (the gap to build — the maker pack):** one kraft folder per maker, paper-first:
1. **Consignment agreement** (1 page: the split, payout day, who insures what, either side
   can end it with a conversation)
2. **Your story card** ("who you are, what you make, where" + photo consent tick-box — feeds
   the honest signage and the consent register)
3. **Shelf label template** (maker name + origin, the pledge does the price talking)
4. Food tier check where it is food (`food-business-setup-roadmap.md`)

**Assets (the gap to name a home for):**
- **Square item catalogue** = products, prices, stock, named per maker (`Maker — Product`).
  This is the asset list that matters daily.
- **One Drive folder per maker** = photos, signed story card, label files. Convention:
  `Shop/Makers/<name>/`. The consent tick lives on the story card, noted in GHL.
- **GHL contact** = the relationship record: offer tags, chat notes, consent note, next action.

**Times (decide once, print on the wall):**
- Drop-off window: one fixed weekly slot (e.g. Thu 3-5pm). Not a booking system, a habit.
- Open hours: weekend/market-day windows to start (locked decision), on the website + door.
- **Maker shifts**: the roster is a printed month on the shop wall + a photo of it in the
  makers' WhatsApp group. Software when paper breaks, not before.

**Sell and pay:** Square records each sale against the maker; payouts weekly or monthly at
the agreed split. The close-the-loop rule from the engagement plan is staff work, not
software: *if it sold, tell the maker that day* (a one-line WhatsApp with a photo beats any
dashboard). Photograph every first shelf placement and send it to the maker — that photo is
what turns a one-off into a regular.

## Job 2 — Workshops, build days, volunteers

**The repeating loop (any event, same five steps):**
1. **Create:** Humanitix — duplicate the template, change the date. Caps for tight rooms
   (18-seat maker session), waitlists when full, free or paid (GST handled).
2. **Invite, circles-first:** crew WhatsApp first, members' email next, public last (if at
   all). An invite asks for hands, not attendance: "we're building the kids' corner, who's in?"
3. **Remind:** Humanitix does it automatically.
4. **Run the day:** check-in on the organiser app; paper come-back / can-help sheet on the
   table (the relationship instrument); a small job for everyone in the first ten minutes.
5. **Close the loop:** photos (consent-checked) to the crew group + one-line personal
   thank-yous; Monday sweep types the paper in, tags skills, hand-moves rungs.

**Volunteers are not a module, they are a lane:** the can-help sheet feeds `role:volunteer`
+ a skills note in GHL; the actual organising happens in the crew WhatsApp; the recognition
happens in person and in the after-story ("Sam and the rocket-stove crew"). A volunteer
roster, like the maker roster, is a printed sheet until paper breaks.

## Job 3 — Members: connected, not managed

The connection engine is already designed and it is not a feed. From the cadence doc:
members hear first, invited into the unfinished, asked for their hands. Staff moves:

| Touch | Cadence | Cost to staff |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest Note (members first, always first) | monthly | 1 hr, Ben |
| Name on the shelf / in the after-story | every event | zero extra (it's the work itself) |
| First-shelf photo sent to the maker | each first | 1 min |
| Work day + a real job on arrival | monthly | the day itself |
| Photo wall + member wall on OUR site | after each day | 30 min, consent-checked |
| Crew WhatsApp | daily, runs itself | moderation ≈ zero at this scale |
| "We read every one" replies | as they come | the Monday sweep |

Belonging here is built by contribution and recognition, not by an app. The website's own
member wall and photo wall are the digital home, on our domain, no login cliff.

### Where the community talks to each other (the peer surface)

**A WhatsApp Community** named The Harvest: one announcement channel (admins post: gate
times, new on the shelf, the ask for trestles) + the crew groups underneath (garden, art
space, kitchen/events, makers). One admin per group. Members already have the app; nobody
learns anything new.

| Conversation | Surface |
|---|---|
| Garden doing, workshop chatter, build-day organising | the relevant crew group |
| One ask, many hands ("who can lend a marquee") | announcement channel → replies in crews |
| Bigger ideas needing a considered yes | `/get-involved?form=idea` → GHL `community-idea` → a human replies |
| Deciding things | the work day: long table, chalkboard, come-back sheet — chat organises, it never governs |
| Memory | the Monday sweep types decisions/ideas into GHL notes; the after-story email reflects ideas back |

**Known limits = the platform reopen triggers:** no threads, weak search, ideas sink,
cross-crew mess at 3+ groups. When members say "I missed it" / "where did that idea go,"
escalate the banked ladder: GHL Communities pilot ($0, a feed with memory) → Circle (trial
sheet ready). Not before the pain has a name.

## Job 4 — Taking money (three tills, never confused)

| Money | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shop sales + maker payouts | **Square** | locked decision; the split auditable per maker |
| Workshop/event tickets | **Humanitix** | GST, caps, waitlists; free events cost nothing |
| Membership fees (when membership becomes paid) | **GHL + Stripe** | checkout exists; AUD/GST fine. Gate: only charge once the value is obvious (shelf, days, first-to-know). Decide tier price post-20-June with real members in the room |
| Venue hire / invoices | **Xero** (ACT side) | not a staff-screen concern |

Rule: a steward never needs to know how the money plumbing works, only which till a thing
goes through: *things on shelves → Square; tickets → Humanitix; belonging → GHL link.*

## The staff week (the whole job, ~4-6 focused hrs + open hours)

- **Mon (30 min):** the sweep — inbox, pipeline next-actions, paper pile typed in, rungs
  hand-moved, three numbers read aloud (members / next-event RSVPs / makers on shelf).
- **Thu (drop-off window):** receive stock, label, Square catalogue update, shelf photo →
  makers' WhatsApp ("new from the neighbourhood").
- **Fri (15 min):** confirm weekend roster (the wall + a photo to the group); any scheduled
  email goes out (Ben).
- **Sat/Sun (open hours):** the till, the room, the conversations. Event days: check-in app
  + come-back sheet.
- **Month-end (30 min):** Square payout run + a one-line "how it sold" note to each maker.

## What this view settles

- **Circle / community platforms: no.** From the staff seat it is a sixth surface that does
  none of the five jobs (sell, pay, roster, check-in, relationship). The belonging job is
  carried by contribution, recognition, WhatsApp, and our own site. Decision doc v2's trial
  stays banked; the trigger to reopen is a named pain (crew chaos, >100 active, paid-tier
  demand), not ambition.
- The platform question was never the hard part. The hard parts are the maker pack, the
  Square till, and protecting the Monday sweep. Those are this month's build.
