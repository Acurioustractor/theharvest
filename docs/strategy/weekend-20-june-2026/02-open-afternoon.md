## The event

Saturday 20 June 2026. Gate **1pm**, pizza **from 5pm**, stay till dark.
9 Gumland Drive, Witta QLD 4552, Jinibara Country. Free, everyone welcome, uncapped.

This is a first opening of an old nursery, not a reopening. The gate has been shut a long
time. Witta has about 1,300 people and nowhere to stop, and no shop in a generation.

## The shape of the day

- **From 1pm. Walk the place.** The gardens, the rammed earth building, the pavilion under
  the pecans. What's here, what's coming.
- **Through the afternoon. Two questions.** What you could grow or make for the shop shelf,
  and how you'd use this space. Leave it on the wall or tell us at the long table.
- **From 5pm. Pizza.** Three doughs, whatever the garden gave. Music. Stay till dark.

Free. No register, no bar. Kids welcome, there is a corner with paper and chalk.

## Open / serve / close checklist

**Open (from 12:30)**
- [ ] Signage out from the gate (see signage below)
- [ ] Toilets and handwash checked and stocked
- [ ] Idea wall up, paper and pens out; the two questions written clearly at the top
- [ ] Long table set for the shop-shelf and feedback conversations
- [ ] Kids corner set with paper and chalk
- [ ] Garden walk route clear and safe; trip hazards flagged
- [ ] Pizza station prepped: oven laid, dough and toppings staged for a 5pm light
- [ ] Parking plan briefed to whoever holds the gate

**Serve (1pm onward)**
- [ ] Welcome and Country at the start of the afternoon
- [ ] Host brings new arrivals into the shape of the day
- [ ] Garden walks running in small groups
- [ ] Two questions captured on the wall and at the long table through the afternoon
- [ ] Oven lit for pizza from 5pm; make-your-own bar staffed
- [ ] Music on for the evening

**Close (after dark)**
- [ ] Idea wall and feedback sheets gathered and kept (do not lose the written record)
- [ ] Shop-shelf conversations written up: who, what they grow or make, contact
- [ ] Oven shut down safely, fire out and cold before anyone leaves
- [ ] Site walked, gardens and building checked, gate secured
- [ ] Quick count by hand: rough turnout, pizza served, ideas left

## Capture for the day

The afternoon is the start of the shop shelf, so the written record is the point. Keep three
things and do not lose them:

- **Shop shelf.** Producers and makers who said yes, with what they grow or make and a way to
  reach them. These feed The Shop pipeline afterwards.
- **Ideas.** Everything left on the wall about the space, the kids' corner, market mornings,
  maker days, the empty rooms.
- **Headcount and dough.** A rough by-hand count of who came and how much pizza went.

GHL tags for the day (from the reconciled doc, unchanged): RSVP "I'm coming" adds
`rsvp-pizza-dinner` and `witta-gathering-2026-06-20`; produce conversations add
`interest:markets`, `role:supplier`, `project:act-hv`; ideas add `idea:witta-2026-06`;
feedback adds `feedback:witta-2026-06`. A yes is not a subscribe, so do not add
`harvest-member` or `harvest-newsletter` from an RSVP.
