# 04. Branding and signage pack (Sat 20 June 2026)

> Created 2026-06-19. The print-and-place layer for the 20 June public open day. It pulls the
> signage list and brand look that already live in `launch-ops-run-sheet.md`
> (the day-of detail) and the shop signage copy in `shop-operating-system.md`,
> and gathers them into one pack so signs can be made and placed by 9:00 on the
> day. Companion to `launch-readiness-20-june-2026.md` (the gates) and
> `content-calendar-june-2026.md` (the comms run). Voice: plain, no
> marketing-speak, no invented numbers. Nothing here is sold on 20 June, so no
> price signage and no register signage are needed for launch day.

## Brand look (use everywhere printed)

- **Style:** the graphite-pencil brand look. Simple and clear over polished.
  Direct and material, not glossy. One sign, one job.
- **Palette** (from DESIGN.md, use sparingly for accents and headings):
  - Shed `#1C1917`
  - Milk `#F5F0E8`
  - Golden Hour `#C4922A`
  - Canopy `#4A6741`
  - Crane `#8B4A2A`
- **Logo:** the v1 wordmark. Dark version on light stock, white version on dark
  stock. Do not invent or generate new brand imagery for the day.
- **Place name:** The Harvest, Witta, on Jinibara Country. Use the place name in
  any welcome copy.

## What is not in this pack

- **No price or register signage.** Nothing is sold on 20 June, so no Square and
  no price boards on the day. Pricing belongs to the ongoing shop.
- **The shop-wide pledge board waits.** The honest-signage pledge board (makers
  keep a stated percent of what you pay) is held until Standard Ledger confirms
  the consignment split. It is a shop sign, not a launch-day sign, and the
  percent must be settled before it prints. The copy lives in
  `shop-operating-system.md`. Do not print it on a guess.
