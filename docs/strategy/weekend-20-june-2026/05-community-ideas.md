## The event

Saturday 20 June 2026. Gate **1pm**, pizza **from 5pm**, stay till dark. 9 Gumland
Drive, Witta QLD 4552, Jinibara Country. Free, everyone welcome, uncapped. Nothing is
sold. It happens after the Witta Market that runs that morning.

This is a first opening of an old nursery, not a reopening. The gate has been shut a
long time. Witta has about 1,300 people and nowhere to stop, and no shop in a
generation. The site was a plant nursery called The Green Harvest for decades. The
Harvest carries the name forward. That lineage is the story to tell on the day.

## The shape of the warmth

Warmth is not an extra thing to run. It is how the three moments are held.

- **Maker morning (private, before 1pm).** Greet every maker by name. A real cup of
  tea, not a paper-cup handout. Introduce makers to each other. Show them the actual
  shelf spot. Tell the Green Harvest story on the walk.
- **Open afternoon (gate from 1pm).** Someone at the gate who is glad you came. The
  garden walk in small groups. The two questions where everyone can reach them. A
  working afternoon, not a performance.
- **Pizza evening (from 5pm).** A shared meal made by hand under the pecans. The fire
  as the warm centre of the room. One honest conversation where the quiet people get a
  say, and it gets written down.

The thread through all three: greet by name, slow the first twenty minutes down, and
make it easy for a stranger to feel like a neighbour by the time they leave.

## The ideas, in three tiers

Prioritised so a small team can read it on the day. **DO NOW** is free and high
warmth and should happen. **NICE TO HAVE** is if there are hands and time. **FUTURE**
is seeds for the next gather, not for 20 June. Each idea is one or two lines, concrete,
runnable. Ideas already held in the three moment files are marked with the file so they
are not double-run.

## The "what should happen next" conversation (so quiet people are heard)

This is the heart of the warmth, and it is held in `03-pizza-evening.md`. The point
here: it is designed so the loudest do not win. Nic runs it for about 45 minutes from
6:45.

- **Gather, do not summon.** Bring people together at the long tables and the fire with
  food in hand, not by calling a meeting.
- **A minute of quiet first.** Everyone writes one thing on a card before anyone speaks.
- **Small clusters share back.** Three or four talk it through, one voice per cluster
  shares, so no one has to address the whole crowd to be heard.
- **Invite by name, kindly.** Nic asks people who have not spoken what they landed on.
- **The wall stays open.** Anyone who would rather write than speak still gets heard.
- **Capture it live, keep every card.** Nothing paraphrased away.
- **Close honestly.** What happens to the notes, and no promise beyond what is true today.
