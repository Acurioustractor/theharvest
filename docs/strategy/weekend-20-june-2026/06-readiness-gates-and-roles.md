## The event

The Opening of The Harvest. Saturday 20 June 2026. 9 Gumland Drive, Witta QLD 4552,
Jinibara Country. It happens after the Witta Market that runs that morning.

A public open day. Uncapped, public, free. Nothing is sold (no register, no Square,
no shop sales). Food is served, not sold. No liquor licence, because nothing is
sold. BYO alcohol is fine.

Three moments in one day: a private maker morning before the gate (`01-maker-morning.md`),
the open afternoon from 1pm (`02-open-afternoon.md`), and the pizza evening from 5pm
(`03-pizza-evening.md`). Host is Nic, who leads the room. Stock owner is Susie. Ben is
present for 20 June and departs overseas 27 June.

## The shape of the day

- **Before 1pm. Maker morning.** A private, invite-only maker session, never publicly
  promoted. The maker community's first gather. Invite makers to the gather, not to a
  finished shop. Tea and water only, nothing sold.
- **From 1pm. Open afternoon.** The gate opens. Garden walk, the two questions on the
  wall, make and fix small things, local conversation. A working afternoon, not a
  performance.
- **From 5pm. Pizza evening.** Make-your-own pizza on set bases, then one clear
  conversation about what should happen next. Stay till dark.

The two questions, on the wall all day: "What brings you through the gate?" and "What
should happen next?" The Green Harvest lineage is real and worth saying out loud: the
site was a plant nursery called The Green Harvest for decades, and The Harvest carries
the name forward.

## Capture and the GHL tag map

The written record is the point of the day, so do not lose it. Keep the shop-shelf
conversations (who, what they grow or make, a way to reach them), the ideas and the
"what's next" cards from the wall, and a rough by-hand headcount and dough count.

GHL tags for the day (from the reconciled doc, unchanged): the "I'm coming" RSVP adds
`rsvp-pizza-dinner` and `witta-gathering-2026-06-20`; produce conversations add
`interest:markets`, `role:supplier`, `project:act-hv`; ideas add `idea:witta-2026-06`;
feedback adds `feedback:witta-2026-06`. **An RSVP is a headcount, a dough count and an
intention only.** It never subscribes anyone to the newsletter, a membership or Mighty,
so do not add `harvest-member` or `harvest-newsletter` from an RSVP. The Mighty inside
room is a human-reviewed first cohort of 8 to 12 people, decided after Saturday, never a
bulk-invite from the door, and there is no membership or Mighty promise made on the day.
Tagging happens in the Sunday review, not live on the day (see
`07-friday-prep-and-sunday-reconcile.md`).

## Signage

Keep it simple and human, in the graphite-pencil brand look, dark logo on light stock,
per `04-branding-and-signage-pack.md`. The full sign-by-sign list and wording live
there. For the gates and roles specifically, the signs that carry a gate or a role are:

- **Gate welcome** with the date and "from 1pm, pizza from five, everyone welcome" (gate
  and check-in role).
- **Parking**, with an arrow (parking and path role, gate 6).
- **Fire and oven keep-clear**, "Keep clear, hot oven and fire", around the oven and any
  fire (safety role, gate 7).
- **Free-night and BYO note**: "Free tonight, nothing for sale. BYO alcohol." (the day is
  served, not sold, so this matters).
- **The two questions**, large, by the wall (idea wall role).
- **Kids area**, sited away from the fire (kids corner role).
- **Allergen labels** on the bar: gluten and dairy. GF base and DF cheese available
  (food / pizza role, gate 3 dependent).

No price signage and no register signage, nothing is sold. The shop-wide pledge board
waits until Standard Ledger confirms the consignment split, so it does not print for the
day.
