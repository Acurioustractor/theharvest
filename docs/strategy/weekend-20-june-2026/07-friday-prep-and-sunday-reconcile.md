## Friday prep

The Friday job is to settle whether card payment runs on Saturday and to set up the desk.

Square is allowed on Saturday only if every box in the run sheet's Friday test gate is
checked by Friday night or before the gate opens. See the
"Friday test gate" section of `harvest-opening-payment-run-sheet-2026-06-20.md` for the
full checklist. If any box is not checked, run free and headcount mode and do not take
payments on Saturday.

Friday checklist:

- [ ] Payment mode decided: free and headcount, support only, or paid food.
- [ ] Square Friday test gate complete, or the decision is free and headcount.
- [ ] Cash policy named, per the run sheet.
- [ ] One payment lead named.
- [ ] One Sunday reconciliation owner named.
- [ ] Gate desk sheet printed, with the consent column unticked.
- [ ] The exact line for gate, food, and host agreed and shared on the team channel.

## Sunday reconcile

The Sunday job is to reconcile what happened and to hold off on any GHL tagging until
the review. See the "Sunday reconciliation" section of
`harvest-opening-payment-run-sheet-2026-06-20.md` for the full checklist.

Sunday checklist:

- [ ] RSVP list checked against walk-ins.
- [ ] Manual desk sheet checked against Square sales.
- [ ] Cash counted and reconciled separately, if cash was used.
- [ ] List of who consented to updates.
- [ ] List of who needs a reply.
- [ ] List of who offered help.
- [ ] GHL tags applied only after the review, and only from the agreed tag set.

Do not auto-tag payments or attendance in GHL during the event. Tagging happens in the
Sunday review, after the tag map is deliberately updated.
