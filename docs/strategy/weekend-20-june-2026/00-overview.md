## North star

The best weekend ever for community: open the gate, feed people, and listen to what this
place should become.

## The event

Saturday 20 June 2026. Gate **1pm**, pizza **from 5pm**, stay till dark.
9 Gumland Drive, Witta QLD 4552, Jinibara Country. Free, everyone welcome, uncapped.

This is the first opening of an old nursery, not a reopening. The site was a plant nursery
called The Green Harvest for decades, and The Harvest carries that name forward. The day
happens after the Witta Market that runs that morning, so some people will have been around
the area all day. Nothing is sold. Food is served, not sold. No liquor licence, BYO alcohol
is fine. Water, tea, coffee and soft drinks are served free.

## The shape of the weekend

Three days, with Saturday in the middle.

- **Friday 19. Prep.** Settle the day, set the desk, place the signs, lay the pizza bar,
  walk the fire and oven safety. Decide the payment mode (for 20 June this is free and
  headcount, nothing is sold). See `07-friday-prep-and-sunday-reconcile.md`.
- **Saturday 20. The three moments.** A private maker morning before the gate, then the
  public open afternoon from 1pm, then the pizza evening from 5pm and one conversation about
  what should happen next.
- **Sunday 21. Reconcile.** Gather the written record, check the RSVP list against who came,
  list who consented to updates, who needs a reply, who offered help. GHL tagging happens
  here, after the review, never live during the day. See
  `07-friday-prep-and-sunday-reconcile.md`.

## The arc of Saturday

Three moments, one day. Times are a plan, not a promise, and they flex with the light and
the crowd. The maker morning is private and is never publicly promoted.

| Time block | Moment | What | Run sheet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10:30 to 12:45 (private, before the gate) | Maker morning | The maker community's first gather. Tea, a walk of the place, the shelf conversation 1:1. A first gather, not a finished shop. | `01-maker-morning.md` |
| Gate 1pm, through the afternoon | Open afternoon | Walk the gardens and the rammed earth building, the two questions on the wall, make and fix small things, local conversation. A working afternoon, not a performance. | `02-open-afternoon.md` |
| From 5pm, till dark | Pizza evening | Make-your-own pizza on three set bases, music, then one clear conversation about what should happen next. | `03-pizza-evening.md` |

The two questions on the wall all day: **"What brings you through the gate?"** and
**"What should happen next?"**

## What success looks like

Plain terms, for the day itself:

- People came through the gate and felt welcome, not sold to.
- The gardens and the building were walked, and people pictured what this place could be.
- Both questions on the wall filled up, and the answers were kept, not lost.
- Pizza got made and shared, and the one evening conversation reached the quiet people too,
  not just the loud ones.
- Producers and makers who want to put something on the shelf were caught by hand, with a
  way to reach them.
- The fire went out cold, the site was walked, the gate was secured, and nobody got hurt.
- We wrote down a rough by-hand count of who came, how much pizza went, and how many ideas
  were left, with no invented numbers.

The day is the start of a conversation, not a finished product. A small, warm, honest day
beats a big staged one.

## Capture for the weekend

The written record is the point of the weekend, so do not lose it. Keep three things across
the day and gather them before anyone packs down:

- **Shop shelf.** Producers and makers who said yes, with what they grow or make and a way
  to reach them. These feed The Shop pipeline afterwards.
- **Ideas and "what's next".** Everything left on the wall and on the cards about the space,
  the kids' corner, market mornings, maker days, the empty rooms.
- **Headcount and dough.** A rough by-hand count of who came and how much pizza went.

GHL tags for the day (from the reconciled doc, unchanged): the "I'm coming" RSVP adds
`rsvp-pizza-dinner` and `witta-gathering-2026-06-20`; produce conversations add
`interest:markets`, `role:supplier`, `project:act-hv`; ideas add `idea:witta-2026-06`;
feedback adds `feedback:witta-2026-06`. **An RSVP is a headcount and a dough count only.** It
never subscribes anyone to the newsletter, a membership or Mighty, so do not add
`harvest-member` or `harvest-newsletter` from an RSVP. Tagging happens in the Sunday review,
not live during the day (`07-friday-prep-and-sunday-reconcile.md`). The Mighty inside room
is a human-reviewed first cohort of 8 to 12 people, decided after Saturday, never a
bulk-invite, and there is no membership or Mighty promise made on the day.

## Signage

Keep it simple and human, in the graphite-pencil brand look, dark logo on light stock. Use
the place name: The Harvest, Witta, on Jinibara Country. The full sign list, one sign per
row, ready to make and place from the gate inward by the morning, lives in
`04-branding-and-signage-pack.md`. Nothing is sold on the day, so no price signage and no
register signage. The free-night and BYO note goes up: everything is free, nothing is sold,
BYO alcohol is welcome.

## The single hardest gate (insurance)

**Public liability insurance is the hard kill-switch. No insurance, no event.** Said plainly:
$20M public liability must be bound in the trading entity's name, with a Certificate of
Currency, before the gate opens. The bind is held up by the entity name not being settled
yet (Harvest Pty vs A Curious Tractor Pty, with Standard Ledger), so the name has to land
first. Owner: Ben and Nic.

**Fallback ladder.** If insurance is uncertain, get a same-day or event binder. If it is
still uncertain, the whole day becomes a closed rehearsal of 10 to 15 named people with no
public surface. This is the owner's call, not a day-of one.
