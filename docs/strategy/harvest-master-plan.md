> Created 2026-05-28. The one integrated plan across all high-need areas (shop,
> communications, event, Pty, insurance, other) with the Year-1 cost basis and a Year-2+
> future outlook. Self-contained for reading end to end. Every dollar figure is sourced;
> the source map sits beside this file in `harvest-master-plan.provenance.md`.
>
> The detail layers stay canonical for their domain: `the-harvest-strategic-plan-notion.md`
> for the full budget, `harvest-build-plan.md` for the full task list (and the Notion
> Actions Harvest view for owners + dates), `ghl-setup-runbook.md` and
> `content-calendar-june-2026.md` for how to build, and `working-capital-plan.md` for
> funding options. This plan does not replace them; it pulls them into one view.
>
> Voice: plain, no marketing-speak, no em-dashes.

## The whole picture

The Harvest opens its garden to members on **Sat 20 June 2026** (Nic-led, Ben present) and
commences trading **1 July 2026** as The Harvest Pty Ltd, site-steward of the lease at
9 Gumland Drive, Witta. Six areas need work: the **shop** (the substance), **communications**
(the engagement engine), the **20 June event** (the moment, with the only immovable dates),
the **Pty entity** (the legal vehicle), **insurance** (the hard gate), and **other** (rent,
outgoings, stewards: the standing cost of being open). Year-1 runs at a **~$20,000 loss by
design**, bridged by a **$20,000 to $30,000 director loan** and the grant pipeline.
Year-2+ scales as the Capital Improvement Fund deploys (Target Rent re-bases to $112,500),
sub-op licence fees + programming + memberships grow, and the trust-capital reserve becomes
available.

## By area

### 1. Shop (the community shared shelf)

**Goal:** a Harvest-run shared shelf at Witta, consignment (about 75 to 80 percent to the
maker), Square POS, stewards plus maker weekend shifts, honest signage. Opens **progressively,
not big-bang**. The commercial cafe and retail is the sublicensed future phase.

**Key tasks** (full list in `harvest-build-plan.md` P1):
- Build shop nurture (spec 6), book-a-chat calendar, maker smart list.
- Confirm consignment % + GST treatment with Standard Ledger.
- Square setup (account, card reader, payouts).
- Food-business tier, signage, first-shelf makers, weekend shift model.

**Costs:**
- **Startup:** permits + signage permit absorbed in **$1,500** (per SP:193, the early-access
  compliance line).
- **Annual operating:** the shop is largely revenue-neutral in Year-1. The 20 to 25 percent
  consignment margin covers card fees and the shop's share of staffing. No separate shop
  line in the budget; absorbed into "programming costs" (~$12K/yr, SP:225) and steward time
  (already costed in §6).
- **To cost (not yet sourced, do not invent):** Square hardware + transaction fees, signage
  design + print, food-tier compliance (per the food-business roadmap).

### 2. Communications (CRM + outreach)

**Goal:** the engagement engine. GHL pipelines + workflows + Conversations + WhatsApp; the
website and content calendar; members-first sequencing.

**Key tasks** (full list in `harvest-build-plan.md` P3):
- Build the five silent receipts (specs 1 to 5), member reconfirm, Conversations inbox +
  LeadConnector app, WhatsApp, custom fields.
- Optional: fix the `hi@act.place` sender (Harvest address).

**Costs:**
- **Startup:** marketing + launch + website **$2,000** (SP:195). Domain + hosting is shared
  ACT infra (existing thoharvest.com); ~$100/mo within the annual comms line (WCP:22).
- **Annual operating:** marketing + website + comms **~$4,000/yr** (SP:226).
- **To reconcile (not yet a separate Harvest line):** the GoHighLevel location is shared
  across ACT / Goods / Harvest; cost-share with ACT for accuracy before external reporting.

### 3. Event / programming (20 June + the year)

**Goal:** the 20 June members' day (maker session 10am to 2pm + community pizza dinner 2pm
till late), then a programming rhythm of workshops, pop-ups, and residencies through the
year.

**Key tasks** (full list in `harvest-build-plan.md` P2):
- Decide B1/B2 seat capacities (the live gate); build B1 + B2 RSVP calendars (due
  ~1 June); Wk4 Makers' invite; Harvest Notes 02 + 03; PL insurance bound (the hard gate);
  pizza lead; extra hands; maker name + photo; real photos.

**Costs:**
- **Startup:** pop-up programming **~$2,000** (Phase A food, caterer coordination, venue
  prep; SP:196).
- **Annual operating:** programming costs (facilitators, materials) **~$12,000/yr**
  (SP:225). Programming revenue **~$30,000/yr** (workshops, pop-ups; SP:212). Net positive
  over the year.
- **20 June specifics:** PL insurance is covered under the annual policy; no separate event
  premium once $20M PL is bound.
- **To cost (not yet sourced):** the make-your-own pizza dinner cost on the day (depends on
  headcount + supplier; capacities pending).

### 4. Pty / entity (the legal vehicle)

**Goal:** The Harvest Pty Ltd registered (May 2026), Standard Ledger engaged on Harvest Pty
scope, Xero set up, sole-trader to Pty cutover **30 June 2026**, ongoing compliance.

**Key tasks** (full list in `harvest-build-plan.md` P4):
- Send the Standard Ledger follow-up (founding-circle money mechanism + consignment % + GST
  treatment).
- Decide founding-circle home (A Kind Tractor vs Harvest Pty); do NOT build the founding-
  circle pipeline until this is settled.

**Costs:**
- **Startup:** incorporation + legal (ASIC, trust setup, shareholder agreement) **$2,000 to
  $5,000** (SP:192). Lease deposit **$4,167** (SP:190).
- **Annual operating:** Xero + accountant (Standard Ledger, includes quarterly BAS)
  **~$5,000/yr** (SP:224). Xero subscription ~$70/mo within that (WCP:20). First-BAS setup
  ~$2,000 to $3,000 (WCP:21).
- **Working-capital bridge (Jul to Oct 2026):** **$20,000 to $30,000 director loan**,
  Division 7A documented (WCP §1, §2 Option A).

### 5. Insurance (the hard gate)

**Goal:** **$20M Public Liability bound in The Harvest Pty Ltd's name** before 1 July 2026
(lease requirement) and before the 20 June members' day (or the event cannot legally
proceed). Plus contents + business pack. Broker shortlist: Clear / NFPIB / Aon.

**Key tasks:** PL insurance bound (`harvest-build-plan.md` P2; the hard gate).

**Costs:**
- **Startup:** insurance Year 1 (PL $20M + contents + business pack) **$5,000 to $8,000**
  (SP:194). The PL premium upfront portion is **~$1,500 to $2,500** depending on payment
  frequency (WCP:18); contents + business pack upfront ~$1,000 to $1,500 (WCP:19).
- **Annual operating:** PL + contents + business pack **~$6,000/yr** (SP:223).

### 6. Other (the standing cost of being open)

**Goal:** keep the lease paid, the lights on, the stewards paid. The fixed cost base under
everything else.

**Costs:**
- **Startup (landlord-funded, NOT Harvest cash):** site-level Capital Improvement Fund
  Phase 1 (garden, Art Space, shared infrastructure) **~$25,000** drawn from the landlord's
  $250,000 Fund (SP:191; lease clause 5). Remaining ~$225,000 sits for Phase 2+.
- **Annual operating (Harvest Pty cash):**
  - Base rent (lease clause 3.1) **$50,000/yr** ($4,167/mo; SP:221, WCP:16).
  - Outgoings + utilities + property insurance (tenant's portion) **~$18,000/yr**
    (SP:222; verify from Sonas per WCP:17).
  - Susie (Community Steward, lighter scope) **~$18,000/yr** (SP:219).
  - Joey (Community Steward, paired with Susie) **~$18,000/yr** (SP:220).
  - Repairs / maintenance + misc operating **~$6,000/yr** (SP:227).

## Overall cost basis (Year-1)

**Harvest Pty cash required to start: ~$18,000 to $22,000** (SP:198). Bridged by a $20,000
to $30,000 director loan, repaid across Year-1 from sub-op licence fees + grants.

**Annual operating (site-steward model, with Joey paired):**
- Revenue **~$117,000** (sub-op cafe $24K + garden centre $3K + programming $30K +
  memberships $18K + Art Space $6K + grants $36K; SP:210 to 216).
- Costs **~$137,000** (stewards $36K + base rent $50K + outgoings $18K + insurance $6K +
  Xero/accountant $5K + programming $12K + comms $4K + repairs $6K; SP:219 to 227).
- **Net: ~$(20,000) loss, by design** (SP:230, 232, 236). Covered by director loan +
  grants.

**Working-capital bridge (Jul to Oct 2026):** **~$30,000** conservative planning number
(WCP §1, line 44).

**Revenue-Share mechanic:** Base rent $50K + 100% of Net Profit, capped at Target Rent
$100,000/yr (lease clause 3.2 via SP:234). Year-1 is below the cap by design, so zero
revenue-share to landlord; Year-2+ scaling pushes Net Profit toward and above the cap.

## Funding

**Primary: director loan ~$20,000 + grant pipeline in parallel** (WCP §3).

- **Director loan** (Ben and/or Nic to Harvest Pty, Division 7A documented; WCP §2 Option A).
  Fastest, lowest friction, repayable from future revenue without diluting equity. Indicative
  interest ~8.25% ATO benchmark (verify; WCP line 64).
- **Grant pipeline target $20,000 to $40,000 landed in Year-1** (SP:147 to 148, WCP §2
  Option D). Candidates: Creative Australia Round 2 (closes 1 Sep 2026), Regional Arts Fund,
  Sunshine Coast Council Community Grants, Queensland Government community facility grants,
  philanthropic trusts (Luff network may open doors).

**Reserved:** trust-capital injection (Knight + Marchesi trusts) held for Year-2 if a stronger
balance sheet is needed for broker / landlord / sub-op credibility (WCP §3, Option C).

**Avoided in Year-1:** ACT Pty Ltd to Harvest Pty inter-entity loan. Adds compliance
complexity without meaningful upside this year; revisit in Year-2 (WCP §3, Option B).

## Future costs (Year 2+)

Directional outlook, sourced where available, flagged where not.

- **Rent re-bases as the $250K Fund deploys.** Each Fund milestone lifts Property Value by
  the amount spent. Full deployment lifts Property Value to **$2.25M** and Target Rent to
  **$112,500/yr** (lease clause 3.1(b) via SP:238, 163). Strategic implication: deploy
  thoughtfully, not maximally. Harvest's upside above the cap pushes further into
  Years 2 to 3.
- **Garden-centre sub-op licence kicks in from Month 7** (Feb 2027): ~$500/mo,
  ~$6,000 annualised in Year-2 (SP:211).
- **Cafe sub-op licence rises with sub-op revenue.** Current placeholder ~$2,000/mo
  pending `sub-operator-licence-skeleton.md` execution and the first sub-op's negotiation
  (SP:205, 210).
- **Programming + memberships scale.** Target: Net Profit grows past the $100K cap so the
  surplus above the cap returns to Harvest Pty (SP:234).
- **Trust-capital reserve (Option C)** available for Year-2 to strengthen the balance sheet
  for larger deals (WCP §3).
- **DGR / Foundation work in Year-2** (March 2027, currently deferred; `TODOS.md`).
- **Year-2 lines that need fresh costing (not yet sourced):** Joey + Susie hour scaling if
  scope grows, any second-site exploration, DGR setup if pursued, full Foundation costs.
