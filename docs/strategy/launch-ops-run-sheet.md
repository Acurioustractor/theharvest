## Open / serve / close checklist

**Open**
- [ ] Toilets and handwashing working, soap and paper stocked
- [ ] Potable water available
- [ ] Fire and oven safety: barrier, extinguisher or water nearby, clear zone
- [ ] Trip hazards, dark spots, unclear paths fixed
- [ ] Signage from the gate in place (see below)
- [ ] First-aid kit located, incident process known by the leads
- [ ] B1 and B2 RSVP lists printed for check-in

**Serve**
- [ ] Cold ingredients kept cold until use (esky or fridge)
- [ ] Handwash and clean surfaces at the pizza bar
- [ ] Allergen labels on the bar (gluten, dairy)
- [ ] Bins and recycling visible, lined, near service

**Close**
- [ ] Oven and fire fully out and cold
- [ ] Leftovers handled (see waste plan)
- [ ] Surfaces wiped, utensils washed
- [ ] Bins out, waste removed
- [ ] Lights, taps, fridge, keys, lock up

## Service flow (fridge, prep, service, bins, leftovers)

- **Cold chain:** ingredients in the fridge or eskies, out only at service.
- **Prep:** topping bowls filled at 1:30, refilled as needed.
- **Service:** guests build their own; the pizza lead manages the oven and the queue.
- **Bins:** lined bins for landfill and recycling at the bar and the gathering area.
- **Leftovers and waste:** edible leftovers offered to guests to take, then to the crew; food
  scraps to on-site compost; packaging recycled; landfill out at close.
