# The Harvest visibility, media, and regional arts plan

Date: 2026-07-09
Place: The Harvest, 9 Gumland Drive, Witta, Jinibara Country
Purpose: turn search visibility, regional media, artists, makers, and small events into one working invitation system.

## The decision

Do not build a content machine.

Build a proof loop:

```text
real story -> article -> social and Google post -> partner share -> small event -> GHL capture -> follow-up -> next story
```

The Harvest ranks by becoming the clearest public record of what is actually happening in Witta.

## What success looks like in 90 days

- People searching for The Harvest, Witta pizza, Witta community garden, local makers, and Sunshine Coast hinterland artists can find useful Harvest pages.
- The Google Business Profile is claimed, active, photographed, posted to, and collecting honest reviews.
- Three to six articles are live and linked from social, Google Business Profile, partners, and media.
- At least three artists or makers have been profiled with consent and useful outbound links.
- One small artist or maker event has run.
- A simple regional arts fellowship one-pager exists and has been sense-checked with three potential partners.
- GHL has captured the people who respond: locals, makers, artists, volunteers, newsletter readers, and event attendees.

## The operating model

```text
Notion plans the story.
Website proves the story.
GHL captures the people.
Google and media help people find it.
Events make it real.
```

## First 30 days

### Week 1: make the base findable

- [ ] Decide whether to reopen `/blog` and article routes now, or publish the first stories through existing pages.
- [ ] Claim or request access to the existing Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Add current photos, correct category, current hours, and first Google Business Profile post once access exists.
- [ ] Prepare article 1: `DIY pizza in Witta`.
- [ ] Prepare one social post and one Google post from the same article.
- [ ] Create the first partner share pack: one image, one caption, one link, one editable sentence.

### Week 2: publish the first proof

- [ ] Publish `DIY pizza in Witta`.
- [ ] Publish or prepare `The Harvest Witta: from old nursery to community garden`.
- [ ] Submit new URLs in Search Console.
- [ ] Post to Instagram/Facebook.
- [ ] Post to Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Ask three real visitors for honest Google reviews after a real visit.
- [ ] Add every response into GHL with the right tag.

### Week 3: bring artists and makers in

- [ ] Choose the first artist or maker profile.
- [ ] Confirm consent, spelling, links, photos, and what they want people to know.
- [ ] Publish the profile.
- [ ] Send the person a share pack and ask them to link or share if useful.
- [ ] Create the first artist or maker table event.
- [ ] Invite 8 to 12 specific people directly.

### Week 4: make the first event produce the next story

- [ ] Run the small artist or maker table.
- [ ] Capture photos, one quote, attendance, and consent.
- [ ] Publish a recap.
- [ ] Send one light pitch to a media or arts channel only after the page is live.
- [ ] Draft the fellowship one-pager.
- [ ] Send the fellowship one-pager to three possible partners for advice, not promotion.

## Social posting rule

Every article becomes five small pieces:

- Instagram/Facebook caption.
- Google Business Profile post or event.
- Newsletter paragraph.
- Partner share pack.
- Recap after the event.

Caption pattern:

```text
[Concrete first line.]

[One real detail.]

[Why it matters in one sentence.]

[One invitation.]
```

## Fellowship concept

Working title:

```text
The Harvest working table fellowship
```

Plain description:

```text
A small regional arts fellowship for Sunshine Coast hinterland artists and makers who want to work with a real place, not a blank gallery.
```

Shape:

- 6 artists or makers.
- 8 to 10 weeks.
- Small stipend.
- One site brief.
- One public work-in-progress moment.
- One published profile.
- One thing left behind: sign, bench, shelf object, wall work, workshop, garden object, sound work, story card, repair, or documented method.
- Final weekend public showing.

Do not announce this publicly until:

- [ ] Insurance and workshop safety are clear.
- [ ] Artist payment budget exists.
- [ ] Rights and consent rules are written.
- [ ] Partner asks are specific.
- [ ] GHL capture is ready.
- [ ] First profiles and events have proven interest.

## Rules

- Real photos before graphics.
- Consent before profiles.
- One clear action per post.
- No generic community language.
- Do not call it a fellowship until the payment and partner shape are real.
- Do not ask for SEO links. Give people a useful reason to share.
- Do not open an empty blog. Publish when the first pieces are ready.
