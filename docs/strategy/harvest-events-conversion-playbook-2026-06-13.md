# Getting members from "joined" to "coming": an events conversion playbook

Written 2026-06-13. The problem: plenty of people are joining the members space, but few are signing up for the weekend events. And new people land on Start Here and never find the event link. This fixes both.

## Why the gap happens

Joining the space is free and easy. Signing up for an event is a second, separate action, and right now it is hidden a click or two deep. Two fixes, used together:

1. Direct links that drop someone straight onto the event sign-up, so there is no hunting.
2. Nudges inside Mighty Networks that pull the people who already joined across to the events.

## 1. Use direct event links everywhere (the core fix)

Every event and every paid plan in Mighty Networks has its own URL. Stop sending people to the space home (harvest-the-network.mn.co), that drops them on Start Here with no clear next step. Send them to the event or plan link instead. A new person who clicks it is walked through join, then pay, in one flow.

Grab three links and keep them handy:
- Friday, the $10 notice board event or plan
- Saturday, the $30 pizza night event or plan
- Saturday morning, the free founding members morning event

Use these as the "get your spot" link in Facebook, in email, and inside Mighty. The space-home link is only for "join and look around," not for booking an event.

## 2. Fix discovery inside Mighty Networks

New people see Start Here first, so the weekend has to live there.

- Pin a short "Book your spot this weekend" block at the top of Start Here, with the three direct links. This alone fixes the "new people never see it" problem.
- Pin the same as a post in What's On and the main feed.
- Send a Mighty broadcast or announcement to all members with the direct links. This notifies and emails everyone already in, the fastest way to convert the people who joined this week.
- Point the new-member welcome (the automatic hello) at the weekend links.
- Check the events show in the member events calendar.

## 3. The three-channel cadence

Each channel does a different job. Facebook brings new people, email converts your warm list, Mighty converts the people already inside.

Facebook (reach and new people):
- Posts with direct event links, not the space home.
- Make a Facebook Event for the weekend for reminders and reach, with the Mighty booking link in the details and in a pinned comment.
- Engagement techniques: a comment-to-get-the-link post ("comment PIZZA and I will send you the link"), then reply with the link, comments lift reach. Behind-the-scenes build photos to create a sense of something happening. Ask members to share and bring a friend. Stories with a countdown. Reply to every comment and message fast, with the direct link.
- Share into the local Witta and Maleny community groups.

GoHighLevel email (your warm list):
- A reminder to members who joined but have not booked: "You are in. Here is how to grab your spot for Friday and Saturday," with the direct links as the buttons. Segment on a tag so it only goes to joiners who have not bought.
- A short "this weekend" nudge one to two days out.

Mighty Networks (convert people already inside):
- The pinned Start Here block and feed post above.
- The broadcast with links.
- Host nudges inside Garden Crew and The Shop Makers ("if you are coming Friday, grab your spot here").
- Lean on the founding-member identity: be one of the first, your Polaroid on the wall. Belonging plus a little urgency converts.

## 4. Watch the one number

Track joins versus event sign-ups. If joins are high and sign-ups are low, the links are still not visible enough or there is friction in the pay step. Push the direct links and the in-space nudges harder before blaming the price.
