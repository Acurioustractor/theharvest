# Harvest opening payment run sheet - 20 June 2026

Created 18 June 2026. This is the simple money plan for The Opening of The Harvest.

## Decision

Public promise:

```text
Free entry. RSVP for headcount. No online payment. No Mighty payment path.
```

Operational option:

```text
Square can be used on the night only if the live test sale and refund pass before the gate opens.
```

If the Square test does not pass, run free/headcount mode and use a paper support sheet for people
who want to help later.

## Why

The old launch run sheet says no Square on 20 June. The newer operating call allows Square only if
it is proved before use. This resolves both:

- Facebook and email do not promise paid food.
- The team can still take card payments on site if Square is working and owned.
- Mighty is not used for checkout.
- Payment never becomes consent to email, membership, or Mighty access.

## Modes

| Mode | Use when | What guests hear | What the team records |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free/headcount | Default mode, or Square not tested | "No payment needed. RSVP helps us count food and parking." | Attendance, food count, offers, consent |
| Support-only Square | Square works, but food should stay free | "If you want to support the place, card support is available at the table." | Payment amount, name if given, consent separately |
| Paid food Square | Square works and Ben/Nic explicitly decide to charge for food | "Card payment is available for food tonight." | Item sold, amount, name if given, consent separately |

Recommended for Saturday:

```text
Free/headcount as the public mode. Support-only Square only if the test passes and one payment lead owns it.
```

## Cash policy

Recommended:

```text
No cash unless Ben or Nic names one cashbox owner.
```

If cash is accepted:

- one cashbox only
- one person owns it
- record every cash payment on the sheet
- count cash at close with two people present
- reconcile cash separately from Square

## Day flow

Before gate:

1. Decide mode: free/headcount, support-only Square, or paid food Square.
2. If Square is used, run the $1 sale/refund test.
3. Put the payment mode on the team WhatsApp or run sheet.
4. Tell gate, food, and host the exact line to say.

At gate:

1. Check RSVP or write walk-in.
2. Count food.
3. Ask if they want updates after today.
4. Do not ask for payment unless paid mode is active.

At food/table:

1. Keep food flow separate from payment queue.
2. If support-only mode, make support optional and low-pressure.
3. If paid food mode, use Square item names only.

Close:

1. Export or screenshot Square sales total.
2. Count cash if used.
3. Photograph the manual sheet.
4. Put sheet and Square total in the Sunday reconciliation folder.
5. Do not import or tag payments in GHL until the Sunday review.

## Sunday reconciliation

Sunday owner checks:

- RSVP list vs walk-ins.
- Manual sheet vs Square sales.
- Cash count, if any.
- Who consented to updates.
- Who needs a reply.
- Who offered help.
