## 🎯 The Strategic Question

> **The lease is signed. What does execution look like between now and 1 July 2026 commencement, and how do we make year one a success?**

### The Opportunity
The only retail/hospitality space in Witta. A platform to activate ACT Farm, demonstrate our values, and build community—while generating revenue. Early access through 30 June 2026 gives us six rent-free months to incorporate Harvest Pty, bind insurance, execute amendments, fit out the kitchen, develop the garden, and soft-launch.

### The Risk
Building value in someone else's asset — and now formally so, since Fund-financed improvements become the landlord's property at lease end (clause 7.4, no clawback). Operational intensity. Capital lock-up without ownership. Execution risk between today and commencement: Harvest Pty must register, $20M PL must bind, amendments must execute, Jinibara engagement must initiate — any slip pushes the 1 July start date.

### The Bet
The Harvest is the **shopfront for ACT's mission**—not an end in itself, but a launchpad for everything we're building. The lease structure (base $50K + 100% of net profit capped at Target) aligns landlord incentive with our profitability, and the $250K landlord-funded improvement pool underwrites the fit-out we couldn't afford alone. The bet now is on execution, not on the opportunity itself.

Full $250K deployment lifts Property Value to $2.25M and Target Rent to $112,500/yr. Strategic implication: Harvest Pty's upside above the cap pushes further into Year 2-3 as Fund deploys. Deploy Fund thoughtfully, not maximally.

## 🛡️ Authorization + Accountability

Year-1 governance framework (interim until community governance structure lands per TODOS D6 — decision by 31 Mar 2027, first meeting by 30 Jun 2027).

### Spend authorization thresholds

| Tier | Threshold | Approval |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Day-to-day operating | ≤ $500 single transaction | Susie (Community Steward) approves |
| Mid-tier | $501 – $2,500 | One director (Ben or Nic) pings in Slack/email; proceed unless declined within 24h |
| High-tier | $2,501 – $10,000 | Both directors sign (Slack ack + Xero approval) |
| Strategic | > $10,000 | Both directors + written rationale recorded; referenced in next quarterly open-book report |
| Improvement Fund milestone | Any | Both directors + landlord approval per clause 5.2 (Sonas responds within 14 days) |
| Sub-op licence | Any | Both directors; values-alignment assessment documented |

Susie's authority inside her threshold is genuine — Year 1 needs her to move without waiting on directors for routine ops.

### Community complaint flow

1. **Intake** (any channel: email, in-person, website form, social): Susie logs within 24h (Notion log).
2. **Acknowledge** (48h max): Susie replies to complainant with "received, under review, response within 7 days."
3. **Review** (7 days max): Susie + applicable director assess. Categories: (a) operational fix — Susie handles, (b) values-level — director-led response, (c) sub-op-related — invokes sub-op licence clause.
4. **Response**: Written response to complainant within 7 days of intake. Summary (anonymised) goes into next quarterly open-book report if systemic or values-relevant.
5. **Escalation**: If complainant dissatisfied with response, independent mediation (mutually chosen third party).

This becomes the seed of community governance — track patterns in complaint log to inform the structure decision by 31 Mar 2027.

### Sub-op values enforcement mechanism

Per sub-op licence template (`thoughts/wiki/operations/sub-operator-licence-skeleton.md`):

- **Quarterly check-in** (Susie + sub-op, agenda: operational + values-alignment assessment against CVF)
- **Values-alignment assessment**: structured against the 4 CVF values (Community-Led, Sustainable, Inclusive, Generous per lease Schedule B amendment). Sub-op self-rates + Susie's observation.
- **Graduated response for misalignment**:
  - **First incident**: Private conversation + 30-day observation period + remediation agreed
  - **Second incident**: Written warning + specific remediation + 60-day cure period
  - **Third incident or unresolved**: Licence termination with 60-day notice; licence clause enumerates this
- **Dispute mediation**: If sub-op disputes assessment, independent mediation via mutually-chosen third party before termination.

**Important:** This is values-alignment accountability, not business-performance accountability. Sub-op's commercial success/failure is their own affair (absent the lease's Permitted Use + CVF frame).

### Directors' weekly rhythm

- **Weekly**: Ben + Nic + Susie 30-min check-in (sub-op pipeline, cash flow vs plan, complaints, grant pipeline, site events upcoming).
- **Monthly**: Full team (incl. Cath) 60-min programming + coordination review.
- **Quarterly**: Open-book report (landlord + community) + retrospective.
- **Annually**: Strategic review + option-exercise decision prep.

### Transparency Rhythm (Open-Book Reports, per lease clause 3.4 + CVF transparency commitment)

Single quarterly report, serves multiple audiences. One writing, two publishing channels.

**Cadence:** Within 30 days of quarter end per clause 3.4. First report covers Jul-Sep 2026 operations, published late Oct 2026.

**Content (template):**

1. **Financials** — Gross Revenue, Operating Costs, Net Profit (per clause 3.4 definitions), Revenue Share calc, cash position vs working-capital-plan forecast, director loan status
2. **Programming** — events delivered, attendance, member signups, qualitative highlights
3. **Sub-operator** — signing status, trading performance (sub-op's disclosure per licence), values-alignment check-in summary
4. **Community** — complaint log (anonymised patterns), open events held, Jinibara engagement progress
5. **Fund deployment** — Improvement Fund drawn this quarter, milestones complete, Property Value recalculation if applicable
6. **Grants** — applications submitted, status, income landed, acknowledgments
7. **Risks + rescue activations** — which risks from Register did we hit, which rescues activated
8. **Next quarter focus**

**Audiences + versions:**

- **Sonas / landlord** (mandatory per clause 3.4): Full report delivered within 30 days of quarter end. Financial sections primary; operational sections context.
- **Community** (public per CVF): Same report, published on website + Notion + newsletter. May redact specific complaint details; patterns + outcomes visible.
- **Sub-op partners** (shared per sub-op licence trust-building): Same report, emphasis on operational and financial-health sections that affect their livelihood.
- **Grant funders** (as required per grant terms): Excerpted sections matching each grant's reporting requirements.

**Why this rhythm matters:**

- Clause 3.4 requires landlord reporting anyway — publishing the same content to community is marginal cost
- CVF transparency commitment (clause 11) becomes concrete rather than aspirational
- Future sub-op candidates can read past reports before signing — trust builder
- Part of the platform-potential pattern for values-aligned community hubs
